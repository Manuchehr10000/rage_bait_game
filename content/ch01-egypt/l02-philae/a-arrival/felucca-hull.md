## What it is

A Nile **felucca**: a shallow wooden hull, pointed at both ends, low freeboard, a tiller at the stern, a few boards for seats. Side on, bow to the right. 65 × 14; the deck the tourist stands on is the top edge.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat a (brings you in) and beat g (leaves without you). The same boat.

## Must be right

- Wooden, planked, tarred below the waterline.
- No motor, no cabin. A felucca is a sailing boat.
- The sail is a separate sprite drawn above it.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

None.

## Sources

Any photograph of an Aswan felucca. Roberts shows the older Nile boats (dahabiya and cangia); the felucca is the modern tourist boat and the right one for a tourist.

## Confidence

Design choice; boat type is correct for tourists.
