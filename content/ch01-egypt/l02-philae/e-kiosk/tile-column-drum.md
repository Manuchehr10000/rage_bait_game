## What it is

A section of a Kiosk column shaft: sandstone, round, with the drum joint lines and a **water stain** from the seventy years the island flooded each year. 16 × 16, repeats vertically.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e, under each capital, and the Kiosk's columns in the far layer.

## Must be right

Sandstone, not grey. Drum joints every tile or two. The stain line at the height the game's water rises to.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

The stain line. See easter-eggs.md.
