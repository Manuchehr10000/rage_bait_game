## What it is

The top of one column of the Kiosk seen from the side: a **composite floral capital** (papyrus umbels, lotus and palm mixed, in the late Ptolemaic and Roman manner), and above it a tall plain **abacus block** that was meant to be carved into a Hathor head and never was. 32 × 8: the sprite is the top slab the tourist stands on; the column drums below are tiles.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e, one on each column. Some give way; all look the same.

## Must be right

- The capital is floral and bell-shaped, not Doric, not a plain slab.
- The abacus block above it is plain. Do not carve Hathor.
- Sandstone, warm.

## Deliberately wrong

Only 8 px tall, so the capital is mostly implied by the tile below it. If a taller sprite is needed we can change the manifest.

## Easter eggs

The plain abacus blocks are the egg: they are unfinished, and have been for 1,900 years.
