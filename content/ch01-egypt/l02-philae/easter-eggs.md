# Easter eggs · Philae

| Egg | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| The last hieroglyph | Beat f, the scaffold wall | Esmet-Akhom's inscription of 24 August AD 394, next to a figure of Mandulis. The game's scaffold sits under it. A close copy of the actual graffito would be the deepest egg in the chapter |
| Roberts' viewpoint | Far layer | The Kiosk on the skyline is drawn from the angle of Roberts' 1838 lithograph |
| The waterline | Beat e, the column drums | Seventy years of seasonal flooding left a visible stain line on the columns. Paint the tide mark on the drums at the height the water rises to in the game |
| Uncarved abacus blocks | Beat e | The plain blocks above the capitals are unfinished Hathor heads. Leave them plain |
| The cofferdam | Beat d | Two rows of steel sheet piles with sand between. That is exactly what was built in 1972 |
