# The crocodile

## What it is

A **Nile crocodile** the moment it moves: the back with its ridged scutes, the tail, the eyes above the water. 32 × 10, drawn 4 px left and 2 px up from the rock it replaces, so the head appears.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat b, first 'rock'. It dives under the tourist. Later the drowning animation shows the jaws.

## Must be right

- Nile crocodile: olive-green to grey-brown, heavy scutes in rows along the back.
- At rest it must match the rock sprite pixel for pixel in the middle; only the head and tail are new.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

Sobek. The Egyptians kept crocodiles at Kom Ombo, 40 km north, and mummified them.

## Sources

Photographs of Nile crocodiles; the Kom Ombo mummies (reference).

## Confidence

Design choice.
