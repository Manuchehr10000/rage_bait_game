## What it is

One of the twenty-two hamadryas baboons that sit along the top of the facade with their arms raised, greeting the sunrise. Baboons scream at dawn, so the Egyptians gave them the job of adoring the sun. Seated, forepaws up, the mane and long muzzle of a hamadryas.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat c, twenty-two copies in a row. One of them throws a date; it is drawn identically. 8 × 11, the game draws it 1 px left and 3 px up from its box.

## Must be right

- Twenty-two, and identical. Pillar 4.
- Hamadryas, not a monkey: heavy mane, dog-like muzzle.
- Seated, both arms raised, palms forward. That pose is the whole point.

## Deliberately wrong

The throwing.

## Easter eggs

Twenty-two exactly. Count them.
