## What it is

The wall the baboons sit on, right of the facade: sandstone with a cavetto cornice and torus, the same bedding as the facade. In reality the baboon frieze runs along the top of the facade itself; the game moves it lower and to the right so the baboons can throw at you. The image is 204 × 120 and runs from cornice top to the ground.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat c. The baboons are separate sprites drawn on the cornice, 8 px apart.

## Must be right

Same stone and same cornice profile as the facade: they must look like one building.

## Deliberately wrong

The position of the frieze. Real baboons are 33 m up.
