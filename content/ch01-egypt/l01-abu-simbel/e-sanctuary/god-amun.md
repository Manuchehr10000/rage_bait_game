## What it is

Amun-Ra of Thebes, king of the gods. Seated, with the **flat-topped crown and two tall plumes**, a curved divine beard, a broad collar, a short kilt, hands on the knees or holding an ankh. 16 × 40.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e, second from the left, first of the three the sun reaches.

## Must be right

- The two tall plumes, straight up, are the identifier. Nothing else looks like them.
- Curved beard (a god's), not straight (a king's).

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

None.
