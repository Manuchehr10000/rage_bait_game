## What it is

Ra-Horakhty, the sun at the horizon, falcon-headed, with the **sun disc** on his head and the uraeus on the disc. Seated, broad collar, kilt, hands on the knees. 16 × 40.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e, rightmost of the four.

## Must be right

- Falcon head with the cheek marking, hooked beak.
- Sun disc, with the cobra.
- Rightmost.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

None.
