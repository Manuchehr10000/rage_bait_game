## What it is

The long wall of the temple's interior as one painting, 384 × 64, without the four gods (separate sprites) and without the Osiride pillars if the designer prefers to paint them separately (there is a separate `osiride-pillar` sprite; if the pillars are painted into this wall, leave that sprite unpainted).

The wall carries the **Battle of Kadesh** in two registers of sunk relief: above, the Egyptian camp and the chariot charge; below, the king's army and the Hittite chariots. At this size it is rhythm, not narrative, but the chariots should be chariots. Two-thirds of the way along, the **dark niche** of the sanctuary: 32 wide, full height, black, with a plain jamb either side. It is where Ptah sits.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e. The corridor the tourist runs along; the floor is at the image's bottom edge; the sun beam sweeps across it.

## Must be right

- Sunk relief, not raised: the figures are cut into the stone and the outlines are shadow.
- Kadesh: chariots with two horses, six-spoked wheels, the king larger than everyone.
- The niche is dark. The game never lights it.

## Deliberately wrong

The real hall and the sanctuary are separate rooms 65 m apart. The game compresses them into one corridor.

## Easter eggs

The Kadesh 'poem' and 'bulletin' texts are on the real wall. A column of hieroglyphs copied from Lepsius would be found by the right teacher.

## Confidence

The battle scene is well documented; its exact layout on this wall should be checked against Lepsius if reproduced closely.
