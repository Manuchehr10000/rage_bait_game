## What it is

The king seated among the gods, as a god. Nemes with uraeus (in the sanctuary he wears the nemes; some restorations show a crown on top), the false beard, hands on the knees. 16 × 40.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e, third from the left.

## Must be right

- Same face as the colossi outside.
- Straight king's beard. The uraeus.
- He is the same size as the gods. That is the point of the sanctuary.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

None.

## Sources

- David Roberts, *Egypt and Nubia* (1846–49), plates "The Great Temple of Aboo-Simbel" and "Front elevation of the Great Temple of Aboo-Simbel". Public domain. The sand still half-buried the colossi when he drew them.
- Giovanni Belzoni, *Narrative of the Operations and Recent Discoveries* (1820), his account and plates of clearing the entrance in 1817. Public domain.
- Lepsius, *Denkmäler aus Aegypten und Aethiopien* (1849–59), Abtheilung I, Blatt 99–101. Public domain.
- Modern photographs for the relocated state: use for reference only, never in the repo.
- Photographs of the sanctuary's four statues (reference only). They are badly weathered; the identifying attributes are still readable.

## Confidence

Well documented; the exact headdress on the weathered statue is less certain than on the colossi.
