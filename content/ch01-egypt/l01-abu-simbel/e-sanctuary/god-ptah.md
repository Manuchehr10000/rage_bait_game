## What it is

Ptah of Memphis, creator god and patron of craftsmen, and a god connected with the underworld. Seated, in a tight **skullcap**, a **straight beard**, wrapped like a mummy with his hands emerging to hold a **was-sceptre** combined with the djed and ankh. 16 × 40, feet on the floor.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e, leftmost of the four, in the dark niche. The sun beam never reaches him.

## Must be right

- Skullcap, not a crown. Straight beard, not curved.
- Mummiform, sceptre held in front with both hands.
- Leftmost. The order is Ptah, Amun-Ra, Ramesses, Ra-Horakhty.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

He stays in the dark on the two solar days. Whether the light truly never touches him is debated (some say it grazes his shoulder); the game takes the popular version.

Attributes well documented. The 'never lit' claim is popular rather than precise.
