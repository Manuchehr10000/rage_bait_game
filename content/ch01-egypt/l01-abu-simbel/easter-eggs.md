# Easter eggs · Abu Simbel

Things a player who knows the site can find or notice. None of them is required to finish. All of them must be right.

| Egg | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| The Belzoni graffito | Facade, high left of the door | Belzoni and his party cut their names into the facade in 1817. It is still there. A faint scratched name at the right height would be found by the right person |
| The join lines | Facade and colossi | The 1968 reassembly left hairline joins between blocks. Extremely faint horizontal and vertical lines in the stone, spaced as the real cuts were (blocks up to 20 tonnes at the face) |
| The rebus | Niche over the door | Ra-Horakhty with the user sceptre and Maat: User-Maat-Ra. See `b-colossi/ra-horakhty-niche.md` |
| Ptah stays dark | Sanctuary | The beam spares only his niche, which is what actually happens |
| The Small Temple | Far layer, left of the start | Nefertari's temple with its six standing colossi, 100 m north. Not in the game yet; would live in the backdrop |
| The concrete dome | Only if the camera ever goes above the plateau | The artificial hill is hollow: a concrete dome over each temple. Not visible in the game today |
