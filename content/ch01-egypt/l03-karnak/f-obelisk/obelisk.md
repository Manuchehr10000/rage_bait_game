## What it is

A standing obelisk in **pink Aswan granite**: a single shaft tapering to a **pyramidion**, one column of hieroglyphs down the visible face (Hatshepsut's names and her dedication to Amun), on a square base. 16 × 96, base at the bottom edge. The game rotates the whole sprite when it tips, so nothing outside the shaft's outline.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat f. It stands; it tips onto a runner; it lies as a bridge.

## Must be right

- Granite, pink, with black flecks: not sandstone.
- The pyramidion is a separate pointed cap; originally sheathed in electrum, so a faint difference in surface is right.
- One column of large, deep hieroglyphs, centred.

## Deliberately wrong

It tips. The real one has stood since about 1470 BC.

## Easter eggs

The base inscription: cut and raised in seven months.
