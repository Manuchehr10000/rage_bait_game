## What it is

The top of one column of the Hypostyle Hall, side on: a **papyrus** capital and the plain **abacus** block above it that the architrave sat on. The nave's twelve columns have the **open umbel** (bell) capital; the aisles' 122 have the **closed bud**. The game draws one sprite for all, so paint the closed bud, which is the majority. 32 × 8: the slab the tourist lands on. The shaft below is tiles.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e, in the dark, one per column. The spotlit ones fall.

## Must be right

- Papyrus, not lotus, not Greek. The bud capital narrows toward the top.
- Sandstone. Traces of paint (the real columns still show red, blue and yellow) are welcome but must be on every copy.

## Deliberately wrong

One capital type for both nave and aisles.

## Easter eggs

Painted colour surviving in the shadows, as it does under the architraves.
