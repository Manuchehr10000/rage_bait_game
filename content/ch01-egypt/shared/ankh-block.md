## What it is

A block floating in the air with an ankh on its face. It is the one thing in the game that is not from the site: it is from a different, more famous game. Hit it from below and something happens. What happens is never good.

## Where it stands in the game

Level 1 near the start (honest: it gives a coin). Level 2 over the landing stage. Level 3 at the very start, where hitting it opens the floor.

## Must be right

- The ankh itself must be drawn correctly: loop on top, a crossbar, a stem, proportions as on a real amulet, not a Christian cross with a loop.
- The block must look like it belongs to the same stone as the level around it, so the joke is the floating, not the material.

## Deliberately wrong

The block itself is the deliberate anachronism. That is the whole joke, so it must be obvious and confident, never half-hidden.

## Easter eggs

In level 3 the block sits over the Karnak Cachette (see that level). The block is the bait; the floor is the trap.

## Sources

Any museum ankh amulet, e.g. faience ankhs in the British Museum. The block is a homage; do not copy the other game's block.

## Confidence

Design choice.
