The tourist sat on the ground, knees up, looking at nothing. This is what pressing R does: the counter ticks 'Gave up' and they sit.

## Where it stands in the game

Any level, when the player gives up. 13 × 14, bottom edge on the floor.

## Must be right

Same costume, same person. The posture is resignation, not rest.
