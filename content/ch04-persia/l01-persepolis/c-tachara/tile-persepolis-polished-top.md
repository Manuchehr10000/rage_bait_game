## What it is

The lintel of a doorway of the Tachara, Darius's palace on the terrace, called the "Hall of
Mirrors" for its stone: the darkest and most highly polished limestone at Persepolis (Iranica,
"Persepolis"; Livius). Its mud-brick walls have gone and its stone door and window frames stand on
their own. A door frame is three monoliths, the lintel and two jambs, about 75 t together. In the
game: one tile of the dark stone, near black-grey, a soft sheen across it, sky over it.

## Where it stands in the game

Over the two doorways on the Tachara's platform, at tiles 50 and 54 of row 1: x 800 to 816 and 864
to 880, y 16 to 32. Its underside is 48 px, about 5.1 m, over the platform at y 80. **Solid**: he
walks under it, and a jump under it knocks his head on it and nothing happens. The two must be
**identical to the pixel** (pillar 4): neither does anything, and neither may look as if it might.

The jamb under it, a block of the same stone from its underside down to the platform, and the
cavetto cornice on top of it are drawn by the game; the king on the jamb is `tachara-jamb-king`.
The game gives this tile to any block of masonry with nothing under it, and in the level only the
two lintels are such blocks.

## Must be right

- The Tachara's stone: the site's limestone at its darkest and most polished
  (`../../CHAPTER.md`, What "accurate" means here). Near black-grey with a soft highlight, lit from
  the upper right: the sun on its top and right edges, its left and lower edges in shade.
- One block: no joint across it.
- It must read as lying on its jamb: the same stone as the jamb under it and the cornice above.

## Deliberately wrong

- **A tile square.** 16 × 16 px, 1.7 m each way, the size the grid allows. The real lintels'
  proportions are not verified.
- **The doorway's height** under it, 48 px (5.1 m), is the game's, chosen so that a jump bumps it.
- **In section.** The game cuts each doorway through, lintel and all, and shows the far jamb behind
  it; the near jamb is cut away (`../LEVEL.md`, "Deliberately wrong").

## Confidence

The stone, the frames standing alone, and the door frame of three monoliths: solid at
search-extract level. **Not verified, so ask before painting:** the lintel's proportions; the
height of a doorway under it; whether each of the Tachara's doorways still carries its lintel
today, and which were set back in place by restorers.
