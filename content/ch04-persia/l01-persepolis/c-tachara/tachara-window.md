## What it is

A window frame of the Tachara, standing alone where its mud-brick wall has gone: one block of the
palace's dark polished limestone, a stepped frame round the opening, and on top an Egyptian
cavetto cornice, its gorge flaring out over a torus. The Tachara has sixteen windows and niches,
each cut from one block; one window block measures 2.65 × 2.65 × 1.70 m and weighs about 18 t
(Iranica, "Persepolis"; Livius). From outside a window and a niche are the same block, so this one
painting is both.

## Where it stands in the game

Two on the Tachara's platform, in the background: the window between the head of the stair and the
first doorway, the block at x 772, and the niche between the two doorways, at x 828. Painted with
its top-left at (770, 50) and at (826, 50): two pixels left of the block and five above it, for
the cornice, which overhangs the block by two pixels a side. Its bottom row is on the platform, at
y 80. The block itself is 25 × 25, 2.65 m square at the game's 9.4 px to the metre. Not solid: he
walks past in front of it. The two must be **identical to the pixel** (pillar 4). That a window and
a niche are the same from outside is true, and the game keeps it.

## Must be right

- One block, with no joint in it.
- The dark polished stone of the jambs and lintels: near black-grey, a soft highlight down its
  right side, lit from the upper right.
- The frame stepped round the opening, a sill at its foot, the cavetto cornice over it.
- Window and niche the same: nothing shows through the opening of either.
- To scale: 2.65 m square is 25 px.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The window's opening is dark.** A window frame standing alone, with no wall round it, would
  show what is behind it through its opening; the back of a niche is stone. The game draws both
  openings dark, so that the window and the niche are one picture.
- **Two, not sixteen,** in a row along the walk. The Tachara's plan, a hall of about 15 × 15 m with
  its windows, niches and doorways round it, is not shown (`../LEVEL.md`, "Deliberately wrong").
- **The cornice sits on top of a block 2.65 m square.** Whether the measured 2.65 m includes the
  cornice is not verified.

## Confidence

Sixteen windows and niches, each one block with a cavetto cornice, and the window block of
2.65 × 2.65 × 1.70 m and 18 t: search-extract level (Iranica; Livius). **Not verified, so ask
before painting:** the frame's profile and how many steps it has; the size of the opening;
whether 2.65 m includes the cornice; whether the windows are open through today, and whether from
the court a window can be told from a niche.
