# Beat a · the Stairs of All Nations

Honest. He walks in off the left edge onto the Marvdasht plain, and at x 96 the stair begins: 63
low steps up to a landing, then 48 more to the terrace, 112 px, about 12 m, over the plain. He
runs up it as he runs on the plain. It is a slope of 1 in 3, drawn step by step, and he never
has to jump, is never slowed and never falls. Nothing here lies, and no fall anywhere in the
level can kill. About 4.9 s from the spawn to the head of the stair.

The terrace is reached only by this stair. Its risers are 10 cm and most of its treads 31 cm
(Iranica; Livius): about 1 in 3, so at the tourist's scale a riser is about a pixel and the
steps are drawn at their real size: 64 on the first flight for the real 63, 48 on the second. Each real flight turns back on itself twice and meets its twin
at the top, in a recess of the terrace's north-west wall; the game has one plane and unfolds it
into one climb (LEVEL.md, "Deliberately wrong"). Before any of it is painted, check the treads
(31 cm on most, 38 or 40 on some) and whether wooden treads or boards cover parts of the stair
today (LEVEL.md, "Not verified").

The plain's earth tiles and the terrace's masonry tiles, each plain and with sky above, have
notes here. The step lines over each flight, the stair's masonry face, the plain beyond, the far
hills and the sky are drawn by the game.
