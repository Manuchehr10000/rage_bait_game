## What it is

One of the pair on the east portal of the Gate of All Nations: a bull with a man's head and
wings, facing east, out of the gate (Livius, "Gate of All Nations"). Assyria called such guardians
lamassu; "these bull-men originated in Babylonia and Assyria, but the Persians had adopted them"
(Livius, "Lamassu", through a search extract). English Wikipedia, also through a search extract,
calls the pair "two Lamassu in the Assyrian style", of colossal size. In the game: in profile, a
tall cylindrical crown, a long beard curled in rows, the wing swept back over the body, four legs,
standing on a plinth on the face of the east portal's pier.

## Where it stands in the game

On the east portal's pier (tiles 40 and 41, x 640 to 672), on its plinth. **Painted facing right**,
east, the way he is going, and not flipped. Its top-left is at (640, 66) and its bottom row on the
plinth's top at y 106. The figure is the right-hand 30 × 40; the left column and the bottom row are
its shadow, down and to the left. The game uses a painting as it is, lit from the upper right.

Not solid: he walks out between the pair. Never animated. The pier and the names on it are the
game's.

## Must be right

- A man's head, bearded and crowned, on a bull's body, with wings. It is not the west portal's
  bull (`gate-bull`).
- **Four legs.** The Assyrian guardians of Nimrud and Nineveh have five, so that they look whole
  from the front and from the side; those of the Gate are said to have four (probable: `arc.md`,
  section 7). Nimrud and Nineveh are excluded from the chapter (`arc.md`, chapter 4), and this
  figure must never be taken for theirs. Do not paint it from the Assyrian pairs in the Louvre or
  the British Museum.
- The crown tall and cylindrical; the beard long, squared and curled in rows; the wing's feathers
  in rows, swept back.
- Facing east, out of the gate.
- Cut in the pier, in the same grey limestone, unpainted. As carved as everything else in the level
  (`gate-bull`).
- Painted as it stands today, once its state has been checked (Confidence). The game's own
  drawing shows the face, crown and beard whole, though the Gate's figures are said to be
  severely worn. That is a stand-in, not a choice, and no model for the painting.

## Deliberately wrong

- **Small.** The figure is 40 px, about 4.3 m with its crown, on a pier of 94 px; the real ones are
  colossal (as `gate-bull`).
- **One, not a pair**, and **flat on the wall, in profile**: as `gate-bull`.

## Confidence

Winged human-headed bulls on the east portal, facing east: solid at search-extract level. Four
legs: probable (`arc.md`, section 7). **Not verified, so ask before painting:** their height, and
how deep they are cut; how worn they are, and how much of each face, crown and wing survives (a
search extract calls the Gate's figures "severely worn down" and "lacking detail"); the crown's
exact form; whether Xerxes' inscription is cut above them.
