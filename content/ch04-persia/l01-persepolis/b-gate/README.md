# Beat b · the Gate of All Nations

Honest, and all of it in depth. On the terrace, Xerxes' gate: he walks between the plain bulls
of the west portal, carved on the passage walls and facing west, through the hall past two
columns running off the top of the screen and the polished black benches along its back wall,
and out between the human-headed winged bulls of the east portal, facing east. Nothing here is
solid, nothing moves, nothing lies. A few names are scratched high on the piers, never legible.
About 2.3 s.

The portals are about 10 m high, and so are the game's. The delegations waited on the benches
(Iranica). More than two hundred foreign visitors cut their names at Persepolis from the
seventeenth century on, most of them here and at the Apadana (Iranica, "Persepolis graffiti").
Of the hall's four columns two or three stand, one of them re-erected by IsMEO with a stone
capital set on it, in a hurry, for 1971 (Motamedmanesh, *Arts* 2016); the game stands two at
their real height, about 16.5 m (155 px), running off the top of the screen like the Apadana's,
and leaves the capital out. Before the columns are painted, check how many stand and which is
the re-erected one (LEVEL.md, "Not verified").

The terrace's surface tiles (with sky above, and the plain fill the level never shows), the bull
and the winged bull have notes here. The column is drawn by the game, every column in the level,
the Gate's two and the Apadana's eight, by one function at different heights; so are the
benches, the piers and the scratched names.
