## What it is

A delegate of the south wing of the Apadana's east stair, walking toward the centre with his gift.
The south wing carries twenty-three delegations of the peoples of the empire, each led by an usher
who holds its leader's hand (Livius), each in its own dress and with its own gifts. In the game
every delegate is this one figure, his gift held out before him.

## Where it stands in the game

In the three registers of the south wing, from the façade's south end (x 1008) to the foot of the
left flight (x 1068). Each group is a cypress, two delegates and the usher at their head, one group
every 24 px, each register's groups shifted 9 px along from those of the register above. The
delegates' figures stand 4 and 10 px after the cypress's, on the register's floor (y 84, 96 and
108, top to bottom), where the painting's bottom row, its shadow, lies; the left column is shadow
too. **Painted facing right**, toward the centre, the way it stands, and not flipped. Every one is
**identical to the pixel** (pillar 4). Not solid; never animated.

Each register is 12 px high, an 11 px field over its floor, and the painting is 12: the figure
fills the field from top to floor, and its shadow row lies on the floor.

## Must be right

- Walking toward the centre: each usher leads his delegation toward the king in the centre
  (Livius; `../LEVEL.md`).
- A gift held out before him.
- One figure for every delegate.
- Unpainted grey limestone, crisp, as carved as everything else on the wall.

## Deliberately wrong

- **One delegate for twenty-three delegations.** At this size a delegation is a few pixels, and
  inventing costumes that could be told apart would be making things up (as the map plate's note
  says of its own registers).
- **Two to a group.** The real delegations are of different sizes.
- **A short stretch of the wing:** a few groups, not twenty-three (`../LEVEL.md`).

## Confidence

Twenty-three delegations on the south wing, each led by an usher holding its leader's hand, and
walking toward the centre: search-extract level (Livius: the usher "leads him towards the king
(represented in the centre of the staircase in the 'audience relief')"). **Not verified, so ask
before painting:** that a cypress stands between each delegation and the next (the map plate's
note marks it as the plate's own, not the research's); how many men each delegation has.
