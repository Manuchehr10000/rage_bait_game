## What it is

The winged disc at the top of the central panel of the Apadana's east stair, between the two
seated sphinxes: "a traditional representation of a winged sun, flanked by two sphinxes" (Livius,
through a search extract). In the game it is small and plain: a disc, two wings spread level, a
short tail below. The winged symbol is a living community's emblem today. **It is never a gag.** It
stays carved where it is; the audience happens below it, and its joke is on the tourist (ruled
2026-10-03, `arc.md` section 4; `../../CHAPTER.md`, ruling 6).

## Where it stands in the game

At the head of the central projection, centred over the blank: its top-left at (1177, 53), the
disc over x 1184. One in the level. Like every carving it is painted one pixel wider and taller
than the figure, for its shadow down and to the left. Not solid. It never moves and never changes,
makes no sound and reacts to nothing, a death included.

## Must be right

- Small and plain. The same stone, the same cut and the same light as every carving on the wall:
  no colour, no gilding, no shine and no glow.
- Nothing in the game points at it. No trap is in it or comes from it; the guards are under the
  band.
- It is never used anywhere else in the game, as an ornament, a marker or an icon.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Its size is the game's, fitted to the panel.

## Confidence

A winged disc between two seated sphinxes at the top of the panel: search-extract level (Livius).
**Not verified, so ask before painting:** its exact form; whether a figure rises from the disc, as
one does from the winged symbol over the relief at Behistun; the shape of its wings and of its
tail. Whatever it turns out to be, the ruling holds.
