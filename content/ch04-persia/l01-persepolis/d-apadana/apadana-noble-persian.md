## What it is

A Persian noble of the north wing of the Apadana's east stair, walking toward the centre: the tall
fluted hat and the long robe falling in folds to his feet. The north wing carries Persian and
Median nobles, and guards, some of them turning back to talk to the man behind (Livius). In the
game the nobles are two figures, this one and the Mede (`apadana-noble-median`), one after the
other.

## Where it stands in the game

In the three registers of the north wing, from the foot of the right flight (x 1300) to the
façade's north end (x 1360): a figure every 8 px, Persian and Mede by turns, each register
starting at a different place. On the register's floor (y 84, 96 and 108), where the painting's
bottom row, its shadow, lies; the left column is shadow too. **Painted facing left**, toward the
centre, the way it stands, and not flipped: the game uses a painting as it is. Lit from the upper
right, though he faces left. Every Persian noble is **identical to the pixel** (pillar 4). Not
solid; never animated.

## Must be right

- Persian court dress: the fluted hat, the long robe. Told from the Mede at a glance, by the hat
  and the robe against the cap and the trousers.
- Facing the centre.
- One figure for every Persian noble.
- Unpainted grey limestone, crisp, as carved as everything else on the wall.

## Deliberately wrong

- **Nobody turns to talk.** On the stair some of the nobles turn back to the man behind them
  (Livius); the game's walk in step, all facing the centre.
- **Persian and Mede by turns, one for one.** The real order is not checked.
- **No guards on the north wing.** The wing carries guards as well as nobles (Livius); the game
  draws the nobles only.
- **A short stretch of the wing** (`../LEVEL.md`).

## Confidence

Persian and Median nobles and guards on the north wing, some turning back to talk: search-extract
level (Livius). **Not verified, so ask before painting:** their order; the Persian hat's shape on
this stair; what each holds; that they walk toward the centre (the map plate's note marks the
walking direction on each wing as the plate's own).
