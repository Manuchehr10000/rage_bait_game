## What it is

A Median noble of the north wing of the Apadana's east stair, walking toward the centre: a rounded
cap, a tunic belted at the waist and reaching to the knee, and trousers. The north wing carries
Persian and Median nobles, and guards (Livius). In the game the Mede and the Persian
(`apadana-noble-persian`) walk by turns.

## Where it stands in the game

As `apadana-noble-persian`: in the north wing's three registers, every other figure, on the
register's floor (y 84, 96 and 108), where the painting's bottom row, its shadow, lies, and the
left column is shadow too. **Painted facing left**, toward the centre, the way it stands, and not
flipped; lit from the upper right. Every Median noble is **identical to the pixel** (pillar 4). Not
solid; never animated.

## Must be right

- Median dress: the rounded cap, the belted tunic to the knee, the trousers. Told from the Persian
  at a glance.
- Facing the centre.
- One figure for every Median noble.
- Unpainted grey limestone, crisp, as carved as everything else on the wall.
