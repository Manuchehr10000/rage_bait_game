## What it is

The usher at the head of each delegation of the south wing of the Apadana's east stair, walking
toward the centre and holding the delegation's leader by the hand (Livius). In the game: one
figure, walking right, his arm reaching back for the hand of the delegate behind him.

## Where it stands in the game

At the head of every group in the south wing's three registers: his figure 15 px after the group's
cypress, his hand meeting the front of the second delegate, who is the delegation's leader
(`apadana-delegate`). On the register's floor (y 84, 96 and 108), where the painting's bottom row,
its shadow, lies; the left column is shadow too. **Painted facing right**, the way it stands, and
not flipped. Every usher is **identical to the pixel** (pillar 4). Not solid; never animated.

## Must be right

- In front of his delegation, leading it, his hand holding its leader's.
- Facing the centre.
- One figure for every usher.
- Unpainted grey limestone, crisp, as carved as everything else on the wall.

## Deliberately wrong

- **One usher, one dress.** The game draws every usher alike; the real ushers' dress, and whether
  it changes from one delegation to the next, is not verified.

## Sources

As `apadana-delegate`.

## Confidence

The usher holding the leader's hand: search-extract level (Livius). **Not verified, so ask before
painting:** the ushers' dress; whether the usher looks ahead or turns back to the man he leads;
the direction the delegations walk (as `apadana-delegate`).
