## What it is

The 'Gave up' pose, what R does: sat on the ground, the beard unclipped and in his lap on his
trousers, his own chin out, looking at nothing. Bottom edge on the floor.

## Must be right

- Resignation, not rest.
- The beard off and in his lap must read at a glance: it is what tells this frame from every
  living one.
- In his own colours, on the ground. It must never be mistaken for the audience's death, which is
  upright, in profile, in the wall and in the stone.

## Deliberately wrong

None.
