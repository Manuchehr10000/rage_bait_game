## What it is

The idle frame with his eyes shut and the beard knocked askew: the clip off the ear we see, the
beard hanging from the other, its square foot slewed. Nothing else changes. Used, stretched and
rotated, by every death but the audience, which cuts the living idle frame into the stone
(`false-beard`). Persepolis has no other death, so level 1 never shows it; the chapter's later
levels will.

## Must be right

- The same costume, the same person, the same own hair as `false-beard-seated`.
- The beard comes askew; it never flies off, and nothing is made of it. A death looks like what
  caused it (pillar 8); the beard is only how he is lying.
