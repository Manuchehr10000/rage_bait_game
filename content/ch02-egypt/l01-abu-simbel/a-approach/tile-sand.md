## What it is

Sixteen by sixteen of the forecourt sand, fully buried: no surface line. Tiles repeat, so the edges must wrap seamlessly with themselves and with `tile-sand-top` above.

## Where it stands in the game

The whole lower bank of level 1, from the start to the pit.

## Must be right

Pale Nubian sand, warm not grey. The real forecourt today is levelled and partly paved; the level keeps it as sand for the approach.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.
