## What it is

The king standing against a square pillar, wrapped as **Osiris**: mummiform body, arms crossed over the chest holding the **crook and flail**, a false beard, and a crown. In the real hall the four pillars on the south side wear the double crown and the four on the north the white crown of Upper Egypt. 12 × 60, feet on the floor.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e, repeated every 48 px down the hall by the game. Because the game repeats one sprite, paint one; the crown difference between north and south cannot be shown unless we add a second sprite later.

## Must be right

- Arms crossed, crook in one hand, flail in the other.
- Mummiform: the body is one wrapped shape, no legs shown.
- The face is Ramesses, calm.

## Deliberately wrong

One crown for all eight, where the real hall has two.

## Easter eggs

The north/south crown difference, if a second sprite is ever added.
