## What it is

Sixteen by sixteen of the artificial hill and the rock around the temple: Nubian sandstone with horizontal bedding, no surface line. Repeats; edges must wrap.

## Where it stands in the game

Everything solid from the pit onward: the cliff you climb onto, the rock around the hall, the plateau.

## Must be right

Must look like the same rock as the facade, because it is. Bedding lines continue across tiles.

## Deliberately wrong

The real hill is concrete domes covered with rubble. We draw rock because you never see the dome.
