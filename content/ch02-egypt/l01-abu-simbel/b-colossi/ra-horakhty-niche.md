## What it is

The falcon-headed sun god standing in the niche over the door, sun disc on his head. In the real niche his right hand rests on a **user** sceptre (an animal-headed staff) and his left holds a small figure of **Maat**, the goddess of truth, with her feather. Read together with the sun disc: User-Maat-Ra, the throne name of Ramesses II. The king carved a pun over his own front door.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat b, inside the facade's niche, 10 × 22, feet on the niche floor.

## Must be right

- Falcon head, sun disc, standing, a short kilt.
- The sceptre in one hand and the Maat figure in the other, however small. This is the detail that earns the level its credibility.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.
