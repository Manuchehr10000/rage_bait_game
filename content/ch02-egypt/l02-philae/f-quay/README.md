# Beat f · the quay and the last hieroglyph

The quay, the mooring posts and the scribe's scaffold are drawn by the game. Nothing happens in this beat; it is there to be looked at.

When painted: the wall behind the scaffold is the **Gate of Hadrian**. The scaffold stands under the graffito of **Esmet-Akhom**, dated 24 August AD 394, beside a figure of the Nubian god **Mandulis**. It is the last hieroglyphic inscription known. A faithful copy of it, small, would be the deepest easter egg in the chapter.
