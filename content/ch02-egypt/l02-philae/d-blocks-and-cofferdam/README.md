# Beat d · blocks and the cofferdam

Nothing in this beat is a painted sprite yet. The numbered blocks use the shared `relocation-block`. The cranes, the sinking bank and the cofferdam (two rows of steel sheet piling with the higher water behind them) are drawn by the game. When the designer reaches this beat, the cofferdam wall, its burst state and the wave are the next things to give ids to; ask and they will be hooked.

What must be right when they are painted: the cofferdam was **steel sheet piling**, driven in two parallel rows with sand pumped between them, about 12 m high. It was not concrete. It never burst.
