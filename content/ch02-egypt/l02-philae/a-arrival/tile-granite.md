## What it is

Grey Aswan granite bedrock, the rock of the cataract. Dark, with feldspar flecks, no surface line. Tiles repeat.

## Where it stands in the game

Landing stages and banks throughout level 2.

## Must be right

Granite is grey-pink with large crystals; not sandstone.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

None.
