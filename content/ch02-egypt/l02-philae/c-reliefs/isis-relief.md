## What it is

A standing figure in **sunk relief** on the bank wall: a goddess or a king, in profile, one foot forward, in the Ptolemaic style (fleshier than New Kingdom work). 12 × 24. Cut into the stone, so it reads as shadow lines. Several are drawn in a row; one of them is the one that moves.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat c, the relief wall. All copies identical. Pillar 4.

## Must be right

- Sunk relief, Ptolemaic proportions: rounded limbs, a soft belly, a large ear.
- Profile face, frontal eye and shoulders, as always.
- If it is Isis: the throne sign on her head. If a king: the double crown. Pick one and keep it.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

A hacked-out face on one relief would be right for Philae, but Pillar 4 forbids differences between copies. If used, every copy gets it.
