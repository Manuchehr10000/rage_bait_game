## What it is

A block of the lake's stone lining, just breaking the surface, that you can stand on. 24 × 8, the waterline at the bottom edge. In the game it sinks under you.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat g, five in a row across the sacred lake.

## Must be right

Dressed sandstone, not a boulder: the lake is lined with cut stone.

## Deliberately wrong

The sinking. The real blocks are the lake's walls and steps.
