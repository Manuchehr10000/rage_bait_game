## What it is

One **talatat**: a small standard sandstone block from the temples Akhenaten built at Karnak for the Aten around 1350 BC, about one cubit long, which later kings dismantled and used as fill inside pylons. Many carry a fragment of the original **sunk relief**: a bit of a ray of the Aten ending in a hand, a piece of Nefertiti, a cartouche. 16 × 16; the game lays them side by side in a stair.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat c, the stair up the pylon face. Every block gives way. Every block looks the same.

## Must be right

- Small and uniform; the whole point of talatat is that they are all the same size.
- The relief fragment is cut off at the block's edges, because the wall it came from is gone.
- Amarna style if any figure shows: long skull, thin neck.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.
