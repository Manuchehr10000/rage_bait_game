## What it is

Coursed sandstone of the first pylon: large blocks, rough unfinished face, no surface line. 16 × 16, repeats.

## Where it stands in the game

The pylon body and the court walls.

## Must be right

Unfinished: no relief, no smoothing. Coursed, with the joints offset each course.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

None.
