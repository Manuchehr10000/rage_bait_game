# Easter eggs · Karnak

| Egg | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| The scarab superstition | Beat d, and the pedestal at the exit | Tour guides tell visitors that walking around the scarab of Amenhotep III seven times brings luck or marriage. In the game the scarab has left its pedestal and come looking for you. The empty pedestal is the real exit |
| 3 October 1899 | Beat e | Eleven columns of the Hypostyle Hall fell on that day. The game drops columns on you. If the falling column count were ever eleven, someone would notice |
| Seven months | Beat f | Hatshepsut's obelisk says on its base that it was cut and raised in seven months. The pyramidion was covered in electrum |
| The Cachette | Beat a | The pit under the ankh block is where 17,000 bronzes came out of the ground |
| The nilometer | Beat g | The stair down to the water at the lake's edge |
| Aten hands | Beat c | Some talatat carry the ends of the Aten's rays, which are hands. One on a block in the stair would be found |
