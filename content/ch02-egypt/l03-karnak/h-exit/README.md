# Beat h · the exit

The turnstile, the trapdoor and the empty pedestal are drawn by the game. When painted, the pedestal is the **scarab's pedestal** from the north-west corner of the sacred lake: tall, square, sandstone, with the scarab missing because it is in beat d chasing you. The turnstile is a modern ticket-gate turnstile of the kind at the site entrance, out of place, standing on paving that is a trapdoor into the Cachette.
