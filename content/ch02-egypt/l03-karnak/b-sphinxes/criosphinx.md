## What it is

A **ram-headed sphinx** of Ramesses II: a lion's body lying on a plinth, a ram's head with the **curled horns** of Amun's ram, a sun disc between the horns on some, and between the forepaws a small standing **figure of the king** in the nemes. Side on, facing right (down the avenue toward the temple). 32 × 20; the plinth is a tile below.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat b, five copies on five plinths. Two are live and turn; all look identical at rest. Pillar 4.

## Must be right

- Ram, not human, not falcon. Curled horns.
- The king between the paws.
- Lion body, tail along the flank.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

The rams were originally about 120 along the avenue to the quay; the ones today were regrouped in modern times.
