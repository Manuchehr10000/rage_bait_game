## What it is

Sandstone paving slabs of the temple courts, laid in a broken pattern, no surface line. 16 × 16, repeats.

## Where it stands in the game

The floor of all of Karnak in the game.

## Must be right

Sandstone from Gebel el-Silsila: warm, coarse. Slab joints, not brick.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

None.
