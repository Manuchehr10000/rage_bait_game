# Chapter 2 · Egypt

Source: `content/research/arc.md`, chapter 2. Dates 2667 BC to AD 30. Runs south to
north along the Nile. Status: locked.

| Level | Site | Place | Period | Built |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Great Temple of Abu Simbel | Nubia | c. 1250 BC, Ramesses II; relocated 1964–68 | yes |
| 2 | Philae, Temple of Isis | Agilkia island, Aswan | Ptolemaic to Roman; last hieroglyphs AD 394; moved 1972–80 | yes |
| 3 | Karnak | Luxor | c. 2000–300 BC, thirty-plus builders | yes |
| 4 | Dendera, Temple of Hathor | Qena | Ptolemy XII to Tiberius | not yet |
| 5 | Saqqara | Memphis necropolis | Step Pyramid c. 2667 BC; Serapeum to Ptolemaic | not yet |

Reserves that pass both gates: Kom Ombo and Edfu, held out only because they lie south
of Karnak and would reverse the walk. Rejected: Giza (three triangles and a plain),
Deir el-Medina (knee-high foundations).

- **Level 1 teaches:** a monument can be in the wrong place; light arrives on a schedule.
- **Level 5 ends on:** Djoser's stepped terraces and the Serapeum's sarcophagus gallery.
  The chapter closes by leaping back to the beginning of monumental stone.
- **Chapter gag:** Abu Simbel and Philae were both cut up and moved in the 1960s–70s.
  The coordinates are right and the monuments are not there. Revealed on the chapter end
  card, never inside a level.
- **Vocabulary:** pylons and courts, colonnades, roof stairs, crypts and squeezes,
  defaced faces.
- **Keep distinct from chapter 3:** stairs are Egypt's. The Aegean's signature is the
  light well and the multi-storey collapse.
- **Fringe claims excluded entirely:** the Dendera "light", Serapeum precision machining.

## The tone

Deadpan and mean. The world lies; the controls never do. Every death looks like what
caused it and nothing reacts. There is no text inside a level. The full rules are in
`PILLARS.md` at the repository root; read them before drawing a trap.

## The tourist

Research: a bad pharaoh headdress over modern clothes. As drawn: a striped bath towel
worn as a nemes, a false beard on an elastic, a loud shirt, shorts, socks and sandals.
Nobody in the game ever mentions it. It never changes the hitbox. The point of the
costume is that everything around the tourist is real and the tourist is not. The more
exact the temples are, the better the joke.

## What "accurate" means here

- The **architecture** is drawn as it stands today unless the level says otherwise:
  Abu Simbel relocated, Philae on Agilkia, Karnak with its unfinished pylon.
- The **sculpture** is drawn as it was made, because the game's statues are the
  finished statues: crowns, beards, cartouches, the family at the king's legs.
- **Modern intrusions** are shown honestly when they are the joke: the numbered
  relocation blocks, the cofferdam, the turnstile, the spotlights of the sound-and-light
  show. They must be as accurate as the temples.
- Every asset note separates **must be right** from **deliberately wrong**. There is no
  third category.
