## What it is

The tourist flat on their back, arms out, beard now fully sideways. One frame. The game stretches it flat for a crushing, sinks it for a drowning, rotates it for a fall, silhouettes it black for a burning and tumbles it for a wave.

## Where it stands in the game

Every death that leaves a body. Drawn where the living sprite was, so keep the same 12 × 16 box and the same footprint.

## Must be right

- Must be the same person and the same costume as the living frames, one to one.
- No blood, no expression beyond deadpan. Pillar 8: a death looks like what caused it and nothing reacts.
- Because it is silhouetted for burning, the outline must be a clean, readable shape on its own.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.
