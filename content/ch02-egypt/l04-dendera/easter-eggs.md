# Easter eggs · Dendera

For the designer. None of it is stated anywhere in the game.

- **He is going up the down stair.** The procession carved on the east stair walks down it,
  because that is the stair the goddess was carried down. Every figure on the wall is facing
  him. A teacher sees it at once; nobody else needs to.

- **The ankh pays before it falls.** Knock it out of the ceiling and the coin still comes out
  of it, the way it did at Abu Simbel. Then the block follows. At Karnak the reward opened
  the floor; here it is the reward itself that comes down.

- **Only the east suite had a zodiac.** The two suites of Osiris chapels are built by the
  same code and look the same to the pixel, which is fair, because from the roof they did.
  The slab that goes off is where the zodiac was, and the slab in the same place on the west
  suite holds because there was never anything under it. The echo is a history lesson with
  no words in it.

- **Gunpowder.** Lelorrain used it, among saws, jacks and chisels, to get the zodiac out of
  the ceiling in 1821. It is the only death in the game caused by a European collector, and
  the label names the thing he took, not him.

- **The wabet.** The court he falls into is where the statue was dressed for the roof. He
  arrives in it by the shortest route anyone ever took.

- **The sun is behind him, and the light comes from in front.** A rising sun shortens the
  shadows it throws, so the first light lands at the far end of the roof, on the kiosk, and
  runs back toward the chapels. Players will flee from it back toward the shadow. The shadow
  is shrinking in the same direction.

- **The chapel of the disc.** The kiosk was where the goddess waited for the first light of
  the year. If he gets there in time, the first light of the year arrives on a man in a bath
  towel and a false beard, standing exactly where she stood. The game says nothing about it.

- **Every other light in the chapter killed you by arriving.** Abu Simbel's sun swept the
  sanctuary; Karnak's spotlights marked the columns that fall. Here the light is aimed at a
  place, and the only thing to do is be there before it.

- **The sockets.** The architraves of the kiosk carry sockets that suggest a timber roof once
  sat on them. The roof is gone; the holes are drawn.
