# Beat e · the kiosk, and the first light of the year

In the south-west corner of the roof stands the kiosk: twelve Hathor columns joined by screen
walls, open to the sky, called the chapel of the disc. On New Year's morning the statue of
the goddess was set in it before dawn, to receive the first light of the year: the union with
the disc. Sockets in the architraves suggest that it once had a timber roof; if it did, it is gone.

The level is seen from the north, so the east is on the left and the sun rises behind the
tourist. **Crossing the far lip of the wabet's court starts the dawn.** The east goes pink
behind him and the stars go out. Then the first light lands on the kiosk — on the place the
ritual aims it — and spreads back across the roof toward him. That is what a rising sun does
to the long shadow of the chapels he has just passed: the far end is lit first.

He has to be over the kiosk's screen wall and standing where the statue stood before the
light arrives. The clean run gets there about a quarter of a second early. Late, the kiosk
goes gold, the light runs back across the roof at him, and he is ash. **Death label: "The New
Year".** Players will run back toward the shadow. The shadow is shrinking in the same
direction.

On time, the level ends, and the sun keeps coming up on a man in a bath towel and a false
beard, standing in the goddess's place. Nobody mentions it.

This is the one trap in the level on a clock, and it is the last (pillar 5).

The Hathor column has its own note. The screen walls are tiles; the architraves and the back
screen wall are drawn by the game.
