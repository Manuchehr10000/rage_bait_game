# Beat d · the west Osiris chapels, and the wabet's court

After a short stretch of open roof, the west suite of Osiris chapels: built by the same code
as the east one, so the same to the pixel. The same court wall, the same court, the same two
rooms, and in the same place on the second room's roof, the same slab.

It holds. **The zodiac was only ever in the east suite.** There was never anything under this
one.

Just beyond the west suite, the roof is open all the way down: the court of the **wabet**,
the "pure place", a small chapel west of the sanctuary with a court in front of it open to the
sky. The golden statue of the goddess was dressed there on the eve of the New Year before it
was carried up to the roof. From the roof it is a shaft fourteen tiles deep, and the chapel is
at the bottom of it.

A player who has learned beat c jumps the slab. From before the slab the jump lands short,
in the court. **Death label: "The wabet".** The way over is to walk across the slab that
held nothing, to the far edge of the rooms, and jump the court from there.

Identical things, one lying, and the one that lies is the one you remember. The echo teaches
something real without a word: the zodiac was on the east side.

The wabet has its own note. The court's walls are drawn by the game.
