## What it is

The wabet, the "pure place": a small chapel raised on steps at the back of its own court,
west of the sanctuary. On the eve of the New Year the golden statue of the *ba* of Hathor was
brought here, purified, anointed and dressed, and then carried up the west stair to the roof.
Its ceiling carries a great figure of the sky goddess Nut, which nobody will ever see in this
game.

## Where it stands in the game

At the bottom of the wabet's court, fourteen tiles below the roof, seen from above by the
tourist who is about to fall into the court or has just jumped it. The ground line is the
bottom edge. It is dark down there; it is not yet dawn.

## Must be right

- Small, raised on steps, with a columned front and a cornice.
- The same sandstone as the temple.

## Deliberately wrong

- Its size, and where it stands in the court, are not checked; the game draws it centred at
  the foot of a four-tile court.
