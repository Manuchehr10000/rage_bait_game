## What it is

Sand and packed earth, the ground inside the precinct between the enclosure wall and the
temple. No surface. 16 × 16, repeats.

## Where it stands in the game

Under the approach, from the east gate to the side door.

## Must be right

- Earth and sand, not paving. Outside the temple and its courts the precinct is ground.
- Wraps with itself and with its `-top` variant.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

Modern photographs of the precinct, for reference only.
