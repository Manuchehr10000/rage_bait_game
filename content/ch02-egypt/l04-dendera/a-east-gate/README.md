# Beat a · the east gate, the flank, the side door

The level opens in the blue hour before dawn, outside the temple, on its east side. The
enclosure of Dendera is a mud-brick wall with stone gateways in it; the tourist walks
through the east one. The wall has come down to a ragged top over two thousand years, and
the gate has not.

Then the flank of the temple: sandstone, rising straight out of the top of the screen. This
is the only place the player sees how tall the building is before he is on top of it, and it
is how the level says what it is going to be about. A side door, three tiles high, leads
into the thickness of the wall and the foot of the stair.

Nothing happens here. It lasts about two seconds.

The gate has its own note. The tiles have theirs. The sky, the stars, the palms and the
desert edge beyond the enclosure are drawn by the game.
