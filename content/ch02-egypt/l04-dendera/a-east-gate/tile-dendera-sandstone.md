## What it is

The stone of the Temple of Hathor, in section: the flank wall, the mass of the building under
the roof, the walls either side of the stair, the chapels on the roof. Dressed, finished,
fine-jointed coursed sandstone. 16 × 16, repeats sideways and under its `-top` variant.

## Where it stands in the game

Most of the level. The tourist spends it on top of this stone or inside it.

## Must be right

- Dressed and finished. This is not Karnak's unfinished pylon (`tile-sandstone`), and must
  not look like it: Dendera is the most complete temple in Egypt.
- Coursed, with the joints offset each course.
- Warm and yellow. One stone per site (`content/README.md`).

## Deliberately wrong

- The walls are shown plain. Every wall face at Dendera is carved. A tile repeats, and a
  carving that repeats every sixteen pixels would be a lie of a different kind.

## Sources

- David Roberts, *Egypt and Nubia*, the Dendera plates (1838). Public domain.

## Confidence

The stone is sandstone. Which quarry it came from has not been checked; paint what the
photographs show.
