## What it is

A stone gateway standing in the mud-brick enclosure wall of the temple precinct: two jambs,
a lintel, a cavetto cornice, and the winged sun disc over the opening. The tourist walks
through it. The painting is the gate alone; the mud-brick wall either side of it is drawn by
the game. The 76 px include the flare of the cornice, 6 px either side of the jambs, so the
jambs are 64 px across outside to outside and the opening between them is 32.

## Where it stands in the game

The first thing in the level, with the ground line at the bottom edge.

## Must be right

- Stone in a mud-brick wall: the gate is the only masonry in the enclosure's line.
- A cavetto cornice with a torus moulding under it, and the winged disc on the lintel.
- Sandstone, the same stone as the temple.

## Deliberately wrong

- The proportions are the game's: the gate is drawn to be walked through by a figure 16 px
  high.
- Which gate it is. Dendera's enclosure has a monumental north gate, of Domitian and Trajan,
  and an east gate; the level uses the east one because it comes in from the east. Its
  builder and decoration have **not been checked**. Ask before painting anything carved on it
  beyond the winged disc, which is on nearly every gate in Egypt.
