## What it is

The same stone with sky above it: the roof slabs, the roofs of the Osiris chapels, the top of
the stair head, the treads of the stair, the paving of the wabet's court.

## Where it stands in the game

Everywhere the tourist stands once he is in the temple. **The zodiac slab in beat c is two of
these tiles in the grid, like every other roof slab; the game takes them out when the charge
goes off.** From the roof, the zodiac was a slab
like any other, because it is carved on the underside. The tile must therefore carry nothing
that could ever tell one slab from another: no crack, no stain, no mark that the game could
place on one slab and not the next (pillar 4).

## Must be right

- A walking surface of large, flat, fitted slabs, worn smooth.
- Wraps seamlessly with itself sideways and with `tile-dendera-sandstone` below it.

## Deliberately wrong

- The roof is plain paving. The real roof has drains running to the lion-headed waterspouts,
  and graffiti; neither is drawn, because a tile repeats.
