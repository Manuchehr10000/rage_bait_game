# Beat b · the Black Frieze

Twenty-five animals — bison, horse, mammoth, aurochs — drawn on the wall in one black
line, in manganese oxide. The first art the tourist meets, and the level's quiet statement
of what is different here: **in this cave the art is paint, and paint is not a floor.**

Two levels of this chapter have taught that a carving holds your weight. Nothing on this
wall does, ever. The frieze is drawn by the game from the level data and is never collided
with.

One hole in the floor to hop, with the trail stopping either side of it. That is the whole
trap in this beat, and it is there to teach the player to read the gaps in the prints
before it matters.
