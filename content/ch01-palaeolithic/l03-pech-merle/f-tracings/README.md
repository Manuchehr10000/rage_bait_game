# Beat f · the ceiling of finger tracings

A passage with the roof four tiles over the floor, and the tracings on it: lines drawn
with the fingers in soft clay by somebody who could reach, with mammoths and a female
figure somewhere in the tangle. The low roof takes a quarter off every jump made in here,
which is the whole reason the beat exists.

Two holes in the floor. Between them, **a slab of the roof comes down** as he enters the
passage — well in front of him, so it is a thing he watches happen and not a thing that
lands on him — and it stays where it falls, four of the five tiles between the holes.

What it takes away is not the floor. It is the run-up. The first hole is now crossed on
to the top of the slab; the second hole has to be crossed from the two strides of floor
past it, and a man who jumps the second hole from up on the slab is jumping with the
tracings a tile over his head. **The only thing he can reach from up there is the stone.**

The stone is in the middle of the second hole, it is the width of a boot, it is the
obvious way over, and it will not take a landing. The hole is jumped whole or it is not
jumped. There are no prints on it.

## Deliberately wrong

The roof of this passage is not coming down, and has not while anybody has been visiting.
The holes, the slab and the stone are level design. The tracings, and the fact that they
are within reach of a standing hand, are not.
