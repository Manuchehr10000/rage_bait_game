# Beat f · the ceiling of finger tracings

A low passage. The ceiling is close enough that a jump gets about three quarters of its
height before the tourist's head is against the rock, which makes every gap in this
stretch a different problem from the same gap anywhere else in the game.

On that ceiling: a tangle of lines drawn with fingers in soft clay, with mammoths and a
female figure somewhere in it if you know where to look. It is the one piece of art in the
cave that is at arm's length, which is exactly why it is the one the level puts over the
player's head.

Two holes in the floor, the prints stopping either side of each. Drawn by the game.
