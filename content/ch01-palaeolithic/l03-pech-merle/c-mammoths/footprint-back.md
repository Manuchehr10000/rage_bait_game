## What it is

The same print as `footprint`, mirrored: the boy going back the way he came. At Pech Merle
the dozen surviving prints run in **two different directions**, and that is a real and
documented feature of the floor, not a reading of it.

## Where it stands in the game

Twice, and only twice, in the whole level: at x 900 and x 918, on the last of the clay
before the floor runs out at the Chapel of the Mammoths. He walked to the edge, he looked
at it, and he turned round. The concrete beside him carries straight on over the hole for
another six tiles and then stops in mid air.

That is the only warning the level gives before its first betrayal, it is entirely honest,
and it is two footprints wide.

## Must be right

- Identical to `footprint` in every respect except direction. If the two read as different
  feet the beat says the wrong thing.
- The same pale calcite crust.

## Deliberately wrong

Nothing. The prints going two ways is the truest thing in this level, and the game's only
liberty is putting them exactly where they would be most useful.

## Confidence

The two directions are documented. **Not verified:** why. Nobody knows, the game does not
say, and the designer must not paint an answer.
