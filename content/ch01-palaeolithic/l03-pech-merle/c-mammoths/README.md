# Beat c · the Chapel of the Mammoths

Seven metres of mammoths and bison in the upper gallery, and the level's first betrayal.

The clay floor runs out. The **concrete does not**: it carries on over the hole for
another six tiles, railed and level and exactly as convincing as it has been for the last
twenty seconds, and then it stops in mid air.

The prints do something else. They come to the edge, and two of them — `footprint-back` —
face the way the boy came, because he turned round. Then they go up onto the clay bank
above and to the right, and run along it to where the floor starts again.

That is the contract of the rest of the level, stated once: **where the concrete and the
prints disagree, the concrete is wrong.** It is stated in two footprints and nothing else.
