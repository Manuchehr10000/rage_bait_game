# Beat e · the Hall of the Discs

The floor here is the floor of the lower gallery, and the way on is **up**: five plates of
calcite growing out of the wall at rising heights, a hundred and thirty pixels of climb,
and two of them are done holding.

It is the one beat in the chapter that goes upward, and it is placed where it is on
purpose: after four hundred pixels of going down, the player has stopped expecting to be
asked.

There are **no footprints in this beat at all**. The boy did not come this way, and after
twenty seconds of following his trail its absence is the loudest thing in the cave.

Art: `calcite-disc`, with its own note.
