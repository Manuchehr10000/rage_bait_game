# Beat e · the Hall of the Discs

The floor is gone for twenty-two tiles and six plates of calcite cross it, at six
different heights, and two of them are done holding.

The plates want a **measured hop**. A full jump off one goes past the next — which is the
jump the chapter has spent two levels training, and the third level taking away.

There are **no footprints in this beat at all**. The boy did not come this way, and after
twenty seconds of following his trail its absence is the loudest thing in the cave.

Art: `calcite-disc`, with its own note.
