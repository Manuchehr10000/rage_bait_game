# Beat e · the Hall of the Discs

Water under pressure came out of a crack in this rock and set into fans of calcite. The
level makes them the way back up: five plates growing out of the wall at rising heights,
climbing about a hundred pixels from the foot of the Bear's Gallery to the passage.

**There are no prints here at all.** Nobody climbed this — after half a minute of a trail
that has been right every time, its absence is the information, and it is the only beat
in the level with none. Everything in this hall has to be learned by doing it.

- **the second snaps** the moment he touches it. It is a bounce, not a ledge.
- **the fourth walks back down the hall** with him on it, back past the third, and lets
  go where nothing that holds is under him: down on to the second, which snaps, or
  straight to the floor of the lower gallery. Either way it is `The lower gallery`.

The fourth is the fifth shelf of the Bear's Gallery turned round. One carried him forward
to safety and would not let go; this one carries him backwards and does. Between them
they make sure that "it moved, so I should stay on it" and "it moved, so I should get off"
are both wrong somewhere, and that the only way to know which is which is to have died on
the other one.

Calcite, not clay, and drawn as calcite: they are a different material from every other
ledge in the level and they behave like a different material, which is the one place in
this cave where what a thing is made of is worth anything.

## Deliberately wrong

Discs are fragile and are never walked on, but they have been growing where they are for
a very long time and they are not about to drop, and none of them has ever moved
sideways.
