## What it is

The floor of the galleries: fine cave clay, brown, soft. It is the reason there are
footprints at Pech Merle at all — a floor that takes an impression and then gets sealed
under calcite keeps it from the Gravettian, c. 25,000 BP, to now.

## Must be right

- **Soft.** It should look like something a bare foot would sink into, not like rock.
- Brown, warmer than the cave rock around it, and darker where it is damp.
- The `-top` surface is walked, so it is smoother than the mass below it.

## Deliberately wrong

Nothing.

## Easter eggs

Every footprint in the level is on this tile and on nothing else. If the clay does not
read as soft, the prints have nowhere to have come from.

## Sources
