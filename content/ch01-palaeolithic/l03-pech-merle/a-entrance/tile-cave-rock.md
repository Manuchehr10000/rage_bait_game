## What it is

Limestone underground: wet, bedded, and much darker than the same stone in daylight. The
`-top` version is the one with a lamp-lit upper edge, used wherever a surface is open to
the air above it.

## Must be right

- **Wet.** Every surface in a live cave has water on it. This is not the dry limestone of
  Cap Blanc and it must not be painted as if it were.
- Bedding planes running roughly horizontal, because that is how this limestone sits.
- Dark. The only light here is on the tourist's hat, and the tile is mostly what the lamp
  does not reach.

## Deliberately wrong

Nothing.

## Confidence

Generic cave limestone; no specific surface is copied.
