## What it is

The poured concrete of the visitor walkway. Pech Merle takes seven hundred people a day
and it takes them on this. Grey, cast in slabs with the joints showing, worn smooth down
the middle by boots.

## Where it stands in the game

The tour route, in three runs. Every one of them stops somewhere that does not help, and
the last eighty pixels of it are the thing that kills you.

## Must be right

- **Modern, municipal, competent.** This is heritage-service infrastructure and it should
  look it: well made, well kept, faintly institutional.
- Cast in slabs with visible joints, not a continuous pour.
- **Worn in the middle.** Seven hundred pairs of boots a day for decades.
- Grey and flat against everything around it. It is the one thing in the cave that is not
  the colour of the cave, and that is what makes the tourist trust it.

## Deliberately wrong

It ends over a hole, twice, and the last run of it collapses. The real walkway is
inspected and has never dropped anybody. The lie is not in how it is painted; it is in
where the level puts it.
