# Beat d · the Bear's Gallery

No tour comes down here. The concrete stops at the Chapel and does not start again until
the last chamber, and for the whole of this gallery the only thing telling the tourist
where the floor is is a line of prints and a lamp that reaches about a body's length.

This is the level's descent: **eight ledges stepping down and to the right**, over a
hundred and twenty pixels of it, with the floor of the lower gallery a long way below.
Every step is a short drop and every space between the ledges is not: those go all the
way down, and the only way through is one stage at a time.

The prints are on every ledge and stop dead at every edge, which is the only warning
there is and is a completely reliable one.

Cave bears wintered in galleries like this and scraped themselves hollows to do it in,
and the nests survive in the clay of a good many French caves. **The hollows here are
harmless**, which is what a bear nest is: a shallow bowl about thirty centimetres deep.
They are scooped into the ledges and drawn, and nothing happens in them. What kills you
is the cave, not the bears.

No new art. The hollows are drawn by the game as a dip in `tile-clay` with a lit rim.
