## What it is

A stalagmite in the lower cave whose shape a visitor reads as a bear sitting up, which is
why the chamber is the Salle de l'Ours. Calcite: pale, wet, banded, a rounded lump with a
smaller lump on top that is the head if you have been told so.

## Where it stands in the game

On the floor of the great hall, before the Camarin. Solid, honest, a hop: walking into it
stops you. It is the only thing in the hall that stands up off the floor, so it is where a
player first notices the lamp is shorter than it was in the tunnel.

## Must be right

- Calcite, not rock: paler than the wall, faintly banded, wet.
- A bear only if you want it to be. Never a carved bear, never eyes.
- Grown from the floor: it widens at the base.

## Deliberately wrong

Where it stands, and that it is on the route.

## Sources

- ArtsLookUp, Gargas: "a stalagmitic concretion in the lower part of the cave, the form of
  which takes on a bear, hence the name of the Salle de l'Ours".

## Confidence

The stalagmite and the name are real. Its shape at this size is a guess and its position
is level design.
