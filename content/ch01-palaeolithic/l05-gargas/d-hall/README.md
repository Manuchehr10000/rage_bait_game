# Beat d · the great hall, and the Camarin

The great hall of Gargas I: wide, five tiles clear, the height of a full jump. Claw marks
where the bears reached up. The stalagmite the guide calls a bear, `bear-stalagmite`, on
the floor: a hop.

Then the Camarin. A hole in the floor three tiles wide and three deep. A full jump clears
it; a tap does not. From the bottom a passage runs back under the floor you came along,
four tiles, to a smooth wall with two engraved animals on it, the bull and the horse,
drawn as pale lines where the surface came off. Going in and coming out costs two or three
seconds of the lamp, more if you look. As you pass over the hole the lamp's spill shows there is a hollow
under the floor. Nothing says what is in it.

**Must be right:** the Camarin is small and smooth-walled and holds most of the cave's
engravings; the Great Bull and Great Horse are its largest; the bears' marks are real.

**Deliberately wrong:** the Camarin is a side chamber, not a pit and a passage under the
floor. The engravings are two beasts at this size, not copies.

The stalagmite has its own note. The claw marks, the hole and the engravings are drawn by
the game.
