# Beat b · the upper cave

Gargas II: narrow, three tiles clear, a clay roof. Lines drawn with the fingers in the
clay, in tangles, and a few painted animals on the wall under them: ibex and bison
silhouettes, as the first part of the visit shows them.

Nothing to jump for thirty tiles, and it is five seconds long. The first time through, the
beat exists so the player watches the cone and does not yet know why it is shorter than it
was. Every time after that it is **the place to put the lamp out**: the floor is flat, the
roof is flat, and five seconds walked in the spill of an unlit lamp is five seconds of
light in hand at the well — the difference between arriving there with a tenth of the
battery and arriving with a third. The fastest run that never touches the switch does not
need to; it only needs to be perfect everywhere else.

**Must be right:** the upper cave is the narrow one; finger tracings on the clay roof; the
painted animals are few, black, and on the wall, not the roof.

**Deliberately wrong:** the animals are generic silhouettes at this size, not copies of
any published figure.

No new art. The finger tracings and the animals are panels drawn by the game.
