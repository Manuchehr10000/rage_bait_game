# Beat a · the upper portal

The hillside, the mouth of the upper cave, and the steel door in it. Daylight, the cool
grey of a north-facing slope. He starts a few steps short of the door, so a retry is not
a walk up the hill, and walks in; the lamp comes on in the door and from that
moment it is running down — but from that moment, too, L puts it out and lights it again,
and a lamp that is out does not run down.

The switch does nothing outside. There is nothing lit to put out. That is the only thing
the level does to introduce it: this is the one level where `L lamp` is on the controls
line, because it is the one level where the lamp runs down, and the first player to press
it in the door finds out what it is for. Everywhere else L does nothing.

**Must be right:** the upper portal is the way in and the lower is the way out; the door
is steel and modern; the day is the same day as the exit's.

**Deliberately wrong:** the lamp's twenty seconds. Nothing on the door starts it, and a
new battery is in it every time he comes back through the door.

No new art. The mouth and the door are drawn by the game.
