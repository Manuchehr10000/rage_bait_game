# Easter eggs · Gargas

For the designer. None of it is stated anywhere in the game.

- **The clock started at the door.** The lamp's thirty seconds run from the steel door of
  the upper portal, not from the first dark. A player who dawdles in the daylight of the
  hillside loses nothing; a player who dawdles in the door already has.

- **The lamp lies about how long it has.** It holds up for most of its life and collapses
  at the end, the way a battery does. A player who judges the well by how the cone looked
  in the tunnel is wrong.

- **The thing under the floor.** As you pass over the Camarin the lamp's spill shows a
  hollow under the hall floor and something drawn in it. Nothing says go in. The engravings
  are the best thing in the cave, and seeing them is exactly what costs the well its light.

- **The fitted path holds.** Pech Merle's concrete lied every time; Rouffignac's lowered
  floor was the one safe place. The three slabs across the Oubliettes are the guided
  tour's, and every one of them holds. What fails is the lamp.

- **Half the hands are short of a finger.** Drawn, never explained. The research keeps four
  hypotheses alive and the level takes no side.

- **One yellow hand.** One of the 137, as counted. It is on the wall near the right-hand
  end, where the day is strongest.

- **A splinter of bone in a crack.** Drawn in one of the four cracks of the wall of hands,
  the size of a matchstick. It is what dated the cave.

- **The only art seen by daylight.** Five levels of headlamps, museum lighting and a train's
  headlight, and the last wall of the chapter is lit by the door.

- **Ferrage.** The eighteenth-century outlaw said to have hidden here is in the notes and in
  no pixel.
