# Beat c · the tunnel

The passage cut for visitors between the two caves, made here as twelve fitted steps down
with the roof following three tiles above each, and a handrail beside them, because the
stairs of the real visit have barriers. At the bottom, the block that first joined the two
caves, if the mediaeval rockfall is true: `roof-block` from Cap Blanc, on its side. A hop.

**Must be right:** the tunnel is artificial and nineteenth-century; the stairs are fitted
and railed; the lower cave is lower.

**Deliberately wrong:** twelve steps of one tile, in a straight line; the block at the foot.

No new art. The steps are the concrete tiles of Pech Merle; the rail is drawn by the game.
