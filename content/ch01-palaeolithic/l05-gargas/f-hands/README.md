# Beat f · the wall of hands, and the lower portal

The last wall of the chapter, seen by the day coming in at the way out. Twenty tiles of it
near the lower portal, inside the reach of the day, so that it is lit from the right,
strongest nearest the door. The day reaches on past the wall, fading, as far as the far
edge of the slab that tips: from there on a spent lamp does not matter. A player who never
touched the switch has no light of his own left by now; one who saved some can still have
it.

The wall: rock with four cracks across it, a splinter of bone pushed into one of them.
About a hundred negative hands on it, the wall sprayed round each hand so the hand is the
bare rock: adults' hands high, children's low; black and red, and one yellow near the
right-hand end. About half of them are short of fingers, and none of them of the thumb: the
commonest pattern is all four fingers short with the thumb whole. Nothing else. There is no
trap on this wall.

Then the lower portal, the exit, and the end of the chapter.

**Must be right:** negative hands, not prints; black and red with one yellow, black at least
as common as red (black is the majority in both inventories of the cave); children's hands
among them; incomplete fingers on many, the thumb whole on every one, all four fingers
short the commonest pattern; no finger left standing alone or in a rude sign (a lone middle
finger, the V, the corna); the hand is the rock colour and the halo is the pigment; the lower
portal is the way out.

**Deliberately wrong:** the daylight reaches it. The count is about a hundred where the
sources say 137 on this wall. The arrangement is the level's.

**Never in a pixel:** why the fingers are short. Amputation, frostbite and disease, and
fingers folded on purpose are all live, and the research forbids stating any of them
(`arc.md`, "Ritual amputation must not be stated as fact").

No new art. The wall and its hands are a panel drawn by the game.

**The wall as drawn today does not meet this.** `drawHands()` in `src/render/scene.ts`
makes more red hands than black (59 red, 45 black, 1 yellow of 105, with the wall where it
stands today), and it shortens fingers at random: all four short, the commonest pattern at
the site, comes up twice in 105, and the random subsets leave a lone full middle finger on 6
hands, the corna on 2 and the V on 4. Until it is fixed, the note is right and the wall is
wrong.
