# Beat f · the wall of hands, and the lower portal

The last wall of the chapter, seen by the day coming in at the way out. Twenty tiles of it
near the lower portal, inside the reach of the day, so that it is lit from the right,
strongest nearest the door. A player who never touched the switch has no light of his own
left by now; one who saved some can still have it.

The wall: rock with four cracks across it, a splinter of bone pushed into one of them.
About a hundred negative hands on it, the wall sprayed round each hand so the hand is the
bare rock: adults' hands high, children's low; red and black, and one yellow near the
right-hand end. About half of them are short of a finger or two, most often the fourth and
the fifth. Nothing else. There is no trap on this wall.

Then the lower portal, the exit, and the end of the chapter.

**Must be right:** negative hands, not prints; red and black with one yellow; children's
hands among them; incomplete fingers on many, the outer fingers most often; the hand is the
rock colour and the halo is the pigment; the lower portal is the way out.

**Deliberately wrong:** the daylight reaches it. The count is about a hundred where the
sources say 137 on this wall. The arrangement is the level's.

**Never in a pixel:** why the fingers are short. Amputation, frostbite and disease, and
fingers folded on purpose are all live, and the research forbids stating any of them (`arc.md`, "Ritual amputation must not be stated as fact").

No new art. The wall and its hands are a panel drawn by the game.
