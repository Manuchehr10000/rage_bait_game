# Beat e · the well of the Oubliettes

Twelve tiles where the floor is gone, all the way down. Across it the fitted path: three
concrete slabs on their stanchions, the middle one a step up, and every one of them holds.
Falling in is `The oubliettes`.

The point of the beat is the lamp. The fastest run there is arrives with a fifth of it left
and loses the last of it on the far rim; a good run arrives with the lamp flickering; a run
that went down into the Camarin arrives with none, and crosses on what a pair of eyes can
see, which is the next slab's near edge and nothing beyond it.

**Must be right:** the well is deep and it is in the lower cave; the path is the modern
visit's, made and railed.

**Deliberately wrong:** the well is not on the route and the route does not cross it on
slabs. The name is the real one.

No new art. The slabs are the walkway pieces of Pech Merle, drawn by the game, and here they
are not lying.
