## What it is

One bison of the frieze, facing right. The tell is the **hump over the shoulders**,
standing well above the line of the back, with the head carried low under it and the
forequarters shaggy and in shadow. A short forward horn. Ochre traces. 40 × 20, back from
x 4 to x 32 at y 3.

Six bison on the real wall.

## Where it stands in the game

Beats b and c, three times: twice carved, once only engraved.

## Must be right

- **The hump.** A steppe bison's forequarters are its whole silhouette and the line from
  hump to rump falls away steeply. If the hump is timid the animal reads as a cow.
- The head is carried **low**, below the shoulder.
- Shaggy forequarters against a smooth rear: the coat change is real and is drawn in the
  Magdalenian record over and over.
- Short horns, forward and up. Not aurochs horns, which are long and lyre-shaped.
- The **back must be a clean level edge 28 px long**, so the hump has to sit behind and
  above that line without breaking it.

## Deliberately wrong

One sprite for all of them. And no relief is a floor.
