## What it is

One horse of the frieze, facing right, back level, head carried low on a thick neck. The
tells are the **upright mane**, standing proud above the line of the back, and the **full
tail** hanging past the rump. Ochre traces on the body. 40 × 20, with the back from x 4 to
x 32 at y 3 as the ledge.

Seven horses on the real wall, the second largest group after the ibex.

## Where it stands in the game

Beats b and c, three times: twice carved, once only engraved. Nothing in the painting may
distinguish those cases.

## Must be right

- The **stiff upright mane** of a Pleistocene horse. Not a flowing modern one.
- Heavy head, short thick neck, deep jaw: the horses of Magdalenian art are ponies.
- The tail hangs and is full to the root; it is not a donkey's tuft.
- Relief, not outline: a light edge along the back and a shadow under the belly.
- The **back must be a clean level edge 28 px long**.

## Deliberately wrong

One sprite for all of them, for the same reason as the ibex. And the same: no relief is
ever a floor.
