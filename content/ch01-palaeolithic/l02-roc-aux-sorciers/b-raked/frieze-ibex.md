# An ibex of the frieze

## What it is

One ibex of the Roc-aux-Sorciers frieze, facing right, the back a level line, the belly
undercut. **The horns are the whole silhouette**: two of them, sweeping back over the
length of the body, heavily **ridged** across their outer curve. A short beard under the
jaw. Traces of **red ochre** in the hollows. 40 × 20; the back, from x 4 to x 32 at y 3,
is the ledge, and the game's collision is that 28 px run and nothing else.

Eight ibex is the largest group on the real wall, which is why this is the sprite the
level uses most.

## Where it stands in the game

Everywhere. Seven times in the raking light of beat b and the flat light of beat c, twice
as the confronting pair of beat d — the second of those drawn facing left — and once more
in Cave Taillebourg, in beat f, as the block that is leaning rather than attached.

Three of the figures in beat c are drawn from these sprites and are **not** ledges: they
were only ever engraved. The painting must be identical in both cases. That is the level.

## Must be right

- **Ridged horns.** Ibex horns carry heavy transverse ridges along the front of the curve,
  and a teacher will check them. They sweep back over the body, not up.
- The **beard**: an ibex has one and a chamois does not.
- Low relief that still reads as relief: a light edge along the back, a shadow under the
  belly, the legs cut shallower than the body.
- **Ochre in the same places on every copy.** What survives on the real wall is traces in
  the hollows, not colour fields.
- The **back must be a clean level edge 28 px long**, from x 4 to x 32 at y 3. The player
  stands on it.

## Deliberately wrong

The real ibex of the frieze differ from one another in size, pose and preservation. The
game repeats one, because a figure that holds and a figure that is only a line have to be
indistinguishable (pillar 4). No relief is 28 px of anything, and none of them is a floor.

## Confidence

The count of eight ibex and their prominence on the wall is documented. The pose here is
the game's, not a copy of any one figure, and is not claimed to be.
