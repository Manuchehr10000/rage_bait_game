# Beat d · the confronting ibex

Two ibex, nose to nose, with the wall blank between them. The gap is 76 px, and a running
jump is about 68: for the first time in the level there is no way to jump it.

What crosses it is their horns. `ibex-horn`, twice, meeting in the middle, and the line of
the horn is the floor. Four pixels of stone over the river, and everything the level has
taught so far says that a thing this thin is not a ledge.

It is. Nothing here gives way, nothing closes, nothing is on a timer. It is the one place
in the level that is kinder than it looks, and it is placed where it is because the beat
either side of it is not.

The pair are drawn from `frieze-ibex`, the second one facing left. A confronting pair is a
real motif of this wall.
