# Easter eggs · Roc-aux-Sorciers

For the designer. None of this is stated anywhere in the game; it is all in the painting.

- **The headlamp is on for the whole sunlit half.** He put it on at Cap Blanc, where it
  was the only light there was, and has not thought about it since. It does nothing at all
  until beat f. Nobody mentions it.

- **The gate of 1955.** The one object in the level that is telling the whole truth: the
  site has been shut since it was classified, and the tourist is on the wrong side of it.
  Paint it as ordinary as possible.

- **The carved face, turned under.** On `fallen-block`, one or two spots of worked surface
  show at the tipped-under edge. The thing everybody came to see is the underside of the
  floor. It should be findable and never pointed at.

- **The women do nothing.** Five figures at the player's own height, in the one stretch of
  the level where standing still drowns you. A history teacher will recognise them at once
  and a player will spend three deaths edging round them.

- **The horns hold.** After Cap Blanc's ten horses, four of which were honest, the frieze
  here is honest all the way through and the thinnest thing in it carries you.

- **The bison that is not there.** One of the three engraved figures of beat c is a bison,
  drawn exactly like the bison two figures earlier that holds. Whoever paints them should
  be able to point at the pair and say which is which only by counting.

- **The ochre.** What survives on the wall is traces in the hollows. The game repeats the
  same patches on every copy of a sprite, which is a lie about the wall and a promise to
  the player: identical things are identical.

- **Dorothy Garrod and Suzanne de Saint-Mathurin** dug this site from 1947 to 1964 and put
  the frieze in front of the world in 1950. Neither of them appears. The level has no
  people in it at all, which is the correct number.
