# Beat c · the frieze, out of the light

The raking light ends. The wall carries on, the figures carry on, and they are drawn from
the same three sprites in exactly the same way — because in flat light that is what they
look like. A carving stops being three-dimensional the moment the sun is not across it.

Eight figures. **Five are sculpture and hold. Three were only ever engraved and are not
there.** Nothing tells them apart: not the animal, not the size, not the position in the
run, not the ochre. The player finds out by walking into the Anglin, and after that by
remembering (pillar 4).

This is the true half of the site, not an invention. Roc-aux-Sorciers carries monumental
sculpture and discreet engraving on the same wall. The level's only liberty is the order
they come in.

No new art. The three engraved figures are `frieze-ibex`, `frieze-horse` and
`frieze-bison`, drawn as decor instead of as ledges, with no shadow under them — as the
carved ones in this stretch also have no shadow.
