# Beat c · the frieze, out of the light

The raking light ends. The wall carries on, the figures carry on, and they are drawn from
the same three sprites in exactly the same way — because in flat light that is what they
look like. A carving stops being three-dimensional the moment the sun is not across it.

Eight figures, a stride apart, and only the first and the last are what they look like.
In order:

1. Sculpture. It holds.
2. Engraving. Not there.
3. Sculpture, and it lets go one second after he lands on it. Crossing at a run, he
   never sees it move. Standing on it, he goes into the river with it.
4. Sculpture, carved two tiles higher than the rest, the one visible difference in the
   run, and it settles into the river from the moment he stands on it, slowly enough to
   jump off, not slowly enough to think about it.
5. Engraving. Not there.
6. Sculpture, polished. It holds his weight and nothing else: whoever stands on it slides
   back off it faster than he can run, onto the engraving before it, which is not there.
   The way past it is to leave it the instant it is touched.
7. Engraving. Not there.
8. Sculpture. It holds.

Nothing tells them apart: not the animal, not the size, not the ochre, and not the height,
which is true of the fourth and says nothing about what it does. The player finds out by
walking into the Anglin, and after that by remembering (pillar 4).

The carving and the engraving are the true half of the site, not an invention.
Roc-aux-Sorciers carries monumental sculpture and discreet engraving on the same wall, and
carved blocks did come off it in the fall that sealed it. The level's liberties are the
order they come in, and the three that move or slide: no figure of the frieze has moved
since the Magdalenian, and none of it is polished.

No new art. The three engraved figures are `frieze-ibex`, `frieze-horse` and
`frieze-bison`, drawn as decor instead of as ledges, with no shadow under them — as the
carved ones in this stretch also have no shadow. The one that lets go, the one that
settles and the polished one are the same three sprites again, unchanged: nothing on the
painting of the polished horse may show a polish, a sheen or a wet patch, and nothing on
the others may show a crack (pillar 4). The polish is a region the game keeps, not a
thing it draws.
