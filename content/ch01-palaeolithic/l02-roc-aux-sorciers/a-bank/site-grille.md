## What it is

A locked metal gate across the way into the shelter: a plain welded frame with vertical
bars and three horizontal rails, set into the rock. Institutional, cheap, and much
repainted. A padlock or a hasp is the only warm colour on it. 14 × 40.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat a, on the bank, to the left of the path. The tourist walks past it and the game says
nothing. It is never collided with and nothing is ever behind it.

## Must be right

- **Twentieth-century ironwork**, not a period reconstruction and not decorative. Mild
  steel, flat bar, welded. The sort of gate a heritage service puts on a monument.
- **Shut.** It has been shut since 1955 and it is not standing open for anybody.
- Rust and repainting: the paint has been renewed over rust more than once.
- It is fixed to the **rock**, not to masonry. There is no building here.

## Deliberately wrong

Nothing, except that a visitor could get past it at all. The site is closed; the tourist
is on the wrong side of the only honest object in the level.

## Sources

- The site's classification as a Monument Historique in 1955 and its closure since:
  Ministère de la Culture, *Les abris sculptés de la Préhistoire*, Roc-aux-Sorciers.
- Any French heritage service site gate, for the ironwork. Reference only.

## Confidence

The closure and the classification are documented. The gate's design is generic: no
photograph of the actual gate was used, and none is needed.
