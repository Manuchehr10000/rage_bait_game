# Beat a · the bank of the Anglin

The honest opening. The tourist walks in along the bank at the foot of a south-facing
limestone cliff, with the river below on his left and the overhang above him. It is broad
daylight and it stays broad daylight for two thirds of the level.

He has the headlamp on. He had it on at Cap Blanc, where it was the only light there was,
and he has not thought about it since. Nothing in this beat comments on that.

One thing is drawn here and nothing happens to it: `site-grille`, the gate that has been
across the way in since the site was classified in 1955. He walks past it because the
level goes that way.

The path stops where the river comes to the cliff. From there the wall is the only way on.
