# Beat f · Cave Taillebourg

The deeper of the two caves, and the end of the level. It goes dark, and for the first
time in eleven hundred pixels the headlamp the tourist has been wearing since Cap Blanc
does something.

What it shows him is wrong. The art in Taillebourg is **engraved**, not carved: line on a
flat wall, nothing on it to stand on, and the game draws it. The floor has fallen in —
this cave had its own rockfalls, and they are what closed it — and across the gap the lamp
picks out one ledge, one carved animal, at exactly the height a man would want it.

It is `frieze-ibex`, and it is a block that came off the roof in the same collapse as the
ones in the river. It is leaning on the wall. It is attached to nothing. It is the only
dishonest thing in the level and the lamp is what hands it to you.

The gap it leans over is 48 px, which is a jump. The way through is to ignore the only
thing you can see.

No new art in this beat.
