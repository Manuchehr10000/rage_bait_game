## What it is

One of the five female figures of the frieze, carved in relief on the wall. She is a body
**from the waist to the knees and nothing else**: no head, no feet, no arms, and the
carving is cut off flat at the top and at the bottom rather than fading out. Broad hips,
thighs turning in, a **pubic triangle** — the detail these carvings are known for and the
reason they are discussed at all. 14 × 20.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e, five of them along the wall above the sinking blocks, at the height of the
tourist's own chest. Nothing whatever happens to them. They are decor: never collided
with, never triggered, never animated. They are there so that the player, who has spent
two levels learning that everything on a wall is about to do something, spends several
deaths being careful around five things that are not.

## Must be right

- **Waist to knees.** The carving begins and ends where it begins and ends. Do not add a
  head, feet, hands or a face, however much the shape asks for them.
- **Cut flat at both ends**, as if the sculptor stopped. Not faded, not broken off.
- The proportions are the frieze's, not a modern figure's: wide through the hips, heavy
  thighs, a small waist above them.
- Relief on limestone: lit along the top edge, shadowed below, the same stone as
  everything else on this wall.
- She is one of five and they are **not identical on the real wall**. If more than one is
  ever painted, vary them — but never in a way that suggests one of them does anything.

## Deliberately wrong

Nothing about her. This is the most accurate object in the level and the only one the
player will be certain is a trap.

## Sources

  the female figures and the composition of the frieze.
- Iakovleva & Pinçon, *Roc-aux-Sorciers: art et parure du Magdalénien* (1997). Reference only.
- Comparanda: the Venus of Laussel and the Magdalenian female figures of Angles are
  discussed together in the literature. Public-domain reproductions only.

## Confidence

Five female figures, life size, cut at the waist and the knees: documented. **Not
verified:** the exact composition of the famous panel and which of the five belong to it.
Ask before painting them as a group.
