# Beat e · the collapse

The roof of the shelter came down at the end of the Middle Magdalenian. It ended the
occupation, it closed Cave Taillebourg next door, and it buried the frieze — which is the
only reason any of it survived to be dug out in 1950. The most destructive thing that ever
happened here is the reason there is anything to visit.

In the level the blocks of that fall are the floor: six of them, lying in the margin of
the Anglin with their carved faces turned under, so what the tourist walks on is the back
of the stone. `fallen-block`, the same sprite six times (pillar 4).

Each of them does one thing, and nothing about any of them says which:

1. **settles**: a second after he stands on it, it goes down into the river.
2. **turns over**: the moment he is on it, it rolls, face up at last, and he goes in.
3. **is wet**: it holds, and drags whoever stands on it back toward the hole the second
   left, faster than he can walk. Off it at once.
4. **holds**: it is a block.
5. **is shy**: it lies a step higher, on its rubble, so it has to be jumped onto, and the
   first jump taken at it from the fourth makes it hop out of reach, once. Back down, it
   holds for good. The answer is to set it off with a hop in place on the fourth, let it
   come down, and then go. It is Cap Blanc's seventh horse again, in stone.
6. **creeps**: stood on, it creeps back along the margin into the fifth, and breaks where
   it meets it. Jumped over from the fifth's lip, it is nothing.

A man walking off the confronting ibex comes down on the first, with his toes over the
second. The crossing is a jump over the second, a jump straight off the third, a hop in
place on the fourth, a wait, a jump onto the fifth, and a jump from its lip to the floor
of the cave. The wait on the fourth is a pause before the last trap of the level, as Cap
Blanc's seventh horse is: pillar 5 is bent here, not kept.

Above them, on the wall at his own height, are the five women: `venus`, five times, doing
absolutely nothing. They are the only thing in the level carved at the scale of the person
looking at them, and stopping to look at them is what drowns you.

**Must be right:** the blocks are from the roof fall that ended the occupation and sealed
the frieze; they lie face down, carved side under; the five women are on the wall above.

**Deliberately wrong:** everything the blocks do. They came to rest in the Magdalenian and
have not moved since. The number of them, their order and their places at the water's
edge are the level's.
