# Beat a · through the wall of hands

He walks in off the left edge (pillar 13) at the foot of Gargas's wall of hands, drawn as
level 5 left it: the cave's own drawing, dark rock, the hands black and red with one yellow,
lit from the right by the day of the lower portal. He walks through it onto the first ledge of
the shaft. The ledge's edge is where the plate begins: he walks off it, and from that frame on
everything is drawn as a Breuil plate, cream paper, black line, red stipple. He lands in the
plate (`../b-shaft/README.md`, stage 1).

Nothing kills here. It is the level's entry and the change of medium, and it is where the
legend is entered on foot through level 5's grand feature (`arc.md`, section 1).

**Must be right:** the wall is Gargas's wall of hands as level 5 draws it, the same hands,
colours and fingers (`../../l05-gargas/f-hands/README.md`; `src/render/hands.ts`): it is the
same wall, and identical things are identical. The plate's look: cream paper, black outline, red
as stipple; no background rock drawn but what the plate's figures need.

**Deliberately wrong:** walking through a wall. The wall of hands is a wall; here it is a door
into the story the twentieth century told about such walls. The hands' arrangement, count and
fingers carry over level 5's deliberate lies unchanged.

**Never in a pixel:** why the fingers are short (`arc.md`, section 7). No teller of hunting magic
is drawn reading the hands; the hands are the way in, not part of the story.

**Open:** how the change of medium is drawn at the ledge's edge, a line or a sweep, is decided
with the art; nothing about it may read as text, a page number or a plate caption (pillar 2).
