# Beat a · the platform

The mouth of the cave, a short concrete platform along the track, a post at each end with
a chain between them, and the train waiting on its rails with its lights off. This is where
the visit begins, and the tourist begins it by stepping over the chain and walking up the
track.

Honest. Nothing here does anything until he is past the end of the platform, and then,
one second later, the train's headlight comes on behind him and starts to move. He will
not see the train again unless he stops.

`train-engine`, `train-car`. The platform, the chain and the rails are drawn by the game.
