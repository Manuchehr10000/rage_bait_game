## What it is

Crushed stone laid on the clay floor of the gallery for the rails to sit on. Grey, angular, dull.

## Where it stands in the game

The floor of the track from the platform to the end of the line. It is the surface the tourist runs on for the whole level; the rails and sleepers are drawn over it.

## Must be right

- Grey stone, not clay and not concrete.
- Angular fragments; it is quarried and crushed, not river gravel.

## Deliberately wrong

That it is there at all in this form. The real track is laid on the cave floor; how the bed is made was not verified. It is drawn as ordinary railway ballast, which is what a small electric line usually sits on.

## Sources

- Grotte de Rouffignac, official site: electric trains since 1959, unique in a decorated cave; they "channel the public, carry the lighting", and the visit reaches about 1 km from the entrance in about an hour.
- https://www.grottederouffignac.fr/index.php/en/guided-tour

## Confidence

The track is real. The ballast is a reasonable guess and is flagged as one.
