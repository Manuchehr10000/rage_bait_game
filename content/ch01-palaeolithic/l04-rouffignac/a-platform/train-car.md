## What it is

One passenger car: an open-sided bench car under a low roof, the kind a cave train tows two or three of.

## Where it stands in the game

Two of them behind the engine, coupled a few pixels apart. The game draws a red tail lamp on the last one. Every car is the same painting.

## Must be right

- Open sides, a roof, benches. Visitors sit; nobody stands on this train.
- The same gauge and height as the engine.

## Deliberately wrong

- Two cars. The real number was not verified.
- The livery, as for the engine.

## Sources

- Grotte de Rouffignac, official site: electric trains since 1959, unique in a decorated cave; they "channel the public, carry the lighting", and the visit reaches about 1 km from the entrance in about an hour.
- https://www.grottederouffignac.fr/index.php/en/guided-tour
