## What it is

The motor unit of the visitors' train: a small electric locomotive with a driver's cab, its headlight on the front, running on a narrow track. It has been running since 1959 and it carries the lighting for the visit.

## Where it stands in the game

At rest at the platform when the level starts, nose just behind the tourist. It sets off one second after he is past the end of the platform and runs at exactly his speed to the end of the line, where it stops. The game draws its headlight beam ahead of it into the dark. Facing right.

## Must be right

- **Electric**, not diesel: no exhaust, no chimney.
- **Small.** This is a narrow-gauge visitor train in a cave, not a railway.
- The headlight on the front, and it is the only light the train needs: the visit is lit from the train.
- Battery or pantograph was not verified; draw neither prominently.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The colour.** Green is a guess. Nothing in the sources gives the livery.
- **Its speed** is a walking man's, so that the level is about not losing ground. The real train is slower still.
- **It stops with its nose at the end of the track**, where a visitor stands. The real one stops near the Great Ceiling and the visitors walk a few metres.

## Easter eggs

There is nobody driving it and nobody on it. The visit carries on without its passengers, on time.

## Sources

- Grotte de Rouffignac, official site: electric trains since 1959, unique in a decorated cave; they "channel the public, carry the lighting", and the visit reaches about 1 km from the entrance in about an hour.
- https://www.grottederouffignac.fr/index.php/en/guided-tour

## Confidence

The train and its date are solid. Its livery, car count and exact form are unverified; the note says so and the designer should check a photograph before painting.
