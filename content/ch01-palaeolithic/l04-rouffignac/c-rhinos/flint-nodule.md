## What it is

A single nodule of flint, weathered out of the wall and lying loose: a lump about a hand across, pale chalky cortex outside, dark glassy flint where it is broken.

## Where it stands in the game

Twelve of them on the track bed through beats b to e. Each is a solid you can stand on and a hop to get past; walking into one stops you dead, and the train does not stop. Two of them, in beat e, sit thirty pixels short of a band of flint in the roof, and the hop over those has to be a tap, or start early: a full jump made at the rock is still in the air under the flint.

## Must be right

- Cortex outside, dark inside: it must read as flint, not as a pebble.
- Angular, not rounded. Nothing has rolled it.
- Small enough to be a hop and large enough to stop a man walking.

## Deliberately wrong

That it lies between the rails of a working train. Flint does weather out of these walls; it is not left on the track.

## Sources

- Show Caves of France, Grotte de Rouffignac: limestone "full of flint nodules", walls "almost vertical, with thick bands of brown flint nodules in almost horizontal layers".
- Bradshaw Foundation: the walls "inlaid with flint nodules randomly protruding and often defining the decorated areas".

## Confidence

The flint is real and this is what a weathered nodule looks like. Its position is level design.
