# Beat c · the three rhinos

The roof is four tiles over the floor now, and the first **band of flint** hangs out of
it: a lip of rock with the nodules along its bottom edge, four pixels above a walking
head. Walking under it is nothing. Any jump under it is the end, and the level says so
here, once, with ninety pixels of clear floor on either side of it.

On the wall above the track, the **Frieze of Three Rhinos**: three woolly rhinoceros in
black manganese, nose to tail, each drawn round a nodule of flint in the wall that stands
in for the shoulder. The nodules in this cave "define the decorated areas": the artists
used them.

Art: `flint-nodule`. The frieze and the band are drawn by the game.
