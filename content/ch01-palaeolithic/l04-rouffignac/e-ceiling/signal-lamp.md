## What it is

A lamp in a steel case on the end of an arm, the arm coming out of the rock above the
last stop board. One eye, lit. When the train is due the arm swings the lamp out across
the track and then takes it back in.

## Where it stands in the game

It is out for a third of a second, and it is out at exactly the height the flint hangs
at: over the head of a man standing at the board, and through the head of a man in the
air. The tourist who runs up to the board and jumps it the way he has jumped the other
three meets it. The tourist who stands at the board and waits for it to go back does
not, and pays a third of a second of the train's gap for the privilege.

## Must be right

- Steel, painted, chipped. The same made-thing vocabulary as the rails and the platform.
- One lamp, not three. This is a cave railway, not a junction.
- The arm reads as the thing that moves; the lamp is just what is on the end of it.

## Deliberately wrong

All of it. There is no signalling on the Rouffignac line, and there is nothing in that
roof but rock and flint. It is the level's one invented machine, and it exists because
the level's currency is seconds and this is the only trap in the game that charges a
price rather than a life.

## Sources
