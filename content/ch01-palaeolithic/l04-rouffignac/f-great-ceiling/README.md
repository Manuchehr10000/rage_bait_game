# Beat f · the Great Ceiling

The roof opens out to seven tiles and the whole of it is animals: the **Great Ceiling**,
sixty-five of them overlapping in every direction, mammoths, horses, bison, ibex,
rhinoceros, drawn by someone lying on his back a metre under the rock. It hangs over a
shaft to the lower galleries. The hole was filled in and the floor lowered so that
visitors could stand under it and look up, and that is the floor the level ends on.

The track ends at the edge of the lowered floor, exactly where the visit ends, and the
train stops with its nose at the end of the track. The tourist who runs off the end and
drops onto the made floor is safe: the one made thing in this cave is the one place the
train cannot reach. The tourist who stops at the end of the track to look at the ceiling —
which is what the end of the track is for — is where the train stops.

This is the last trap and it is the only one in the level that waits for you (pillar 5).
It is not a cycle; it is a train that was always going to arrive, at a time you set.

No new art. The ceiling, the step and the end of the rails are drawn by the game.
