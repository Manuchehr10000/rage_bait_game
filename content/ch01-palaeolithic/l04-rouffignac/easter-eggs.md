# Easter eggs · Rouffignac

For the designer. None of it is stated anywhere in the game.

- **The train is on time.** It runs at exactly the tourist's speed, so its distance behind
  him is the distance he had when it set off, minus everything he has wasted since. A
  player who watches the light on the left edge of the screen is watching their own
  mistakes add up.

- **The light arrives before the train.** The headlight reaches two hundred pixels up the
  track and the tourist's lamp reaches seventy-six. For the whole middle of the level the
  brightest thing ahead of him is coming from behind.

- **Two kinds of scratch.** The bears' marks curve, are dark, and come in fours. The names
  are straight, pale, in rows, and underlined. They are on the same wall in beat b, and in
  beat d the names are across the mammoths, which is where they really are.

- **The one made floor is the safe one.** Pech Merle's concrete lied every time. Here the
  floor that was dug and filled for visitors is the only place the train cannot follow.

- **The train stops where the guide would stop you.** At the end of the track, under the
  Great Ceiling, where everybody looks up.

- **Nobody is on the train.** The visit carries on without its passengers, on time.

- **Four centuries of visitors and 1956.** Belleforest wrote it down in 1575 and it took
  until 1956 for anyone to say what it was. That story is in the notes and in no pixel.
