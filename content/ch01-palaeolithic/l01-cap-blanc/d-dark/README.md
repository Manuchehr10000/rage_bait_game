# Beat d · the dark

Nothing in this beat is a new painting. The five dark horses use `horse-relief`, the same
sprite as the lit five; the darkness and the headlamp cone are drawn by the game.

Three of these five do something no relief can do: the sixth walks forward out from under
anyone who stands on it, the eighth comes up on its front legs and throws you back the
way you came, and the tenth breaks in the middle and lets you through, one step short of
the far floor. The seventh and the ninth hold. All ten are painted once, and nothing in the painting may hint at which is
which: that is pillar 4, and it is the whole level.

What must be right when the chapter is painted: the beam starts at the lens on the hat
brim, so the lens must sit at sprite column 10, row 4, of the right-facing hiker (see
`../../shared/hiker.md`). The lamp is off until he is through the door in beat b.
