# Beat d · the dark

The five horses beyond the museum's lamps, and one block of the overhang.

Nothing here hints at what any horse does. They are all `horse-relief`, the same sprite as
the five in the light, and the darkness and the headlamp cone are drawn by the game.

| Horse | What it does |
|---|---|
| 6 | Holds for six seconds, then goes. The only place to stand in the dark, and it is on a timer |
| 7 | Jumps when you jump at it from the sixth. Once. Then it comes back down to where it was carved and holds for good |
| 8 | Comes up on its front legs and throws you back the way you came |
| 9 | Breaks in the middle |
| 10 | Holds. Nothing happens on it at all, which after the other four is its own kind of joke |

The seventh is the only puzzle in the level rather than a trap: you cannot cross it until
you have made it jump and let it settle, and the sixth is where you wait while it does. It
answers a jump made from the sixth and nothing else, so a jump anywhere earlier leaves it
exactly as it looks. Six seconds is long enough to do the dance twice and not much more.

What must be right when the chapter is painted: the beam starts at the lens on the hat
brim, so the lens must sit at sprite column 10, row 4, of the right-facing hiker (see
`../../shared/hiker.md`). The lamp is off until he is through the door in beat b. The
block of the overhang belongs to the next beat, on the far floor: see
`../e-dig/roof-block.md`. No lamp hangs past the fifth horse. The far floor is lit because
the dark ends at its edge, not because anything is over it.
