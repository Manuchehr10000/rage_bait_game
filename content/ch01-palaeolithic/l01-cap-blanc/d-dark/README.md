# Beat d · the dark

Nothing in this beat is a new painting. The five dark horses use `horse-relief`; the
darkness and the headlamp cone are drawn by the game from the hiker's lamp. What must
be right when the chapter is painted: the beam starts at the lens on the hat brim, so the
lens must be at the front of the hiker's head sprite (see `../../shared/hiker.md`).
