## What it is

The wall built across the mouth of the shelter to close it, seen from the valley side: rubble limestone laid in rough courses and mortared, 32 world px wide and 128 tall from the underside of the overhang to the ground, with a plain wooden-framed **door** 16 wide and 40 tall, its left edge 8 px in from the wall's left edge. The door is open and dark. The way into the level.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat b. The hiker walks through the door; the shelter interior begins on the other side.

## Must be right

- Rubble masonry, not ashlar, not brick. Local limestone.
- A plain door, no signage, no ticket window. The visitor centre is elsewhere.
- The wall meets the overhang at the top; it is built against rock.

## Deliberately wrong

The real closure has changed over the years and the current covered visitor space is larger than one wall. The game keeps one wall and one door.

## Confidence

The wall exists and is rubble; its date is tradition. Paint it plainly.
