## What it is

A workman of 1909: flat cap, shirt with the sleeves rolled, braces or a waistcoat, trousers, boots, and a **pick**. Stands at the right of the frame facing left, working the deposit to his left. Two frames: pick raised over the shoulder, pick down in the deposit. 32 × 28, feet on the bottom row. He does not look up. He is busy and competent; the tourist is the idiot.

## Where it stands in the game

Beat e, at the edge of the deposit. The swing runs on a cycle once the hiker reaches the far floor; the strike is deadly in the deposit's edge. The last trap in the level, so it is allowed to cycle.

## Must be right

- 1909 working clothes of the Périgord: cap, no helmet, no high-visibility anything.
- A pick, not a mattock, not a trowel. The frieze was found with a pick.
- Both frames the same man, same clothes; only the arms and the pick move.

## Deliberately wrong

He is still there. He is also the only person the tourist ever meets in the chapter, and he never reacts.

## Easter eggs

None beyond himself.
