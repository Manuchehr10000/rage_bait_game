# Beat e · the far floor

The platform at the end of the crossing, lit end to end. The dark stops where this floor
starts, which is why you can see it from halfway along the frieze and why you jump for it
too hard. Nothing hangs over it: no lamp is drawn here, and none should be painted in. The
light is the daylight of the shelter mouth, and the museum's lamps are behind you, over
the frieze.

One thing happens here. A block of the overhang comes down a stride in from the near edge,
and stays where it lands as something to climb: `roof-block.png`, with its own note. It
lets go the moment you are over the edge, so a full jump off the tenth horse puts you
exactly where it lands, and two strides off a short hop walk into it. The way through is
to hop short, stand still at the very edge, and let the roof have its moment.

Beyond it is the deposit the excavation left in place (`tile-sediment`), and the way out
over the top of it.
