# Beat f · the way out

The far floor, the exit label and the light over it are drawn by the game. Nothing to
paint yet.

**The deposit gives way once.** Two tiles of it, a few strides short of the exit (named
from the dev ruler as cap-blanc (1250, 16)), are the whole column of sediment with nothing
holding it: a sixth of a second after he stands on them they go, and they are not under
him while they go, so he goes down into the dig with them (`The trench`). They are drawn
tile for tile as the deposit around them, through the same sediment tile (pillar 4). The
whole jump that climbs the step of the deposit comes down on them; the way out is a short
hop up the step and a jump over them from their lip. It is the last trap of the first
level, and the one that teaches that the floor after a trap is not safe either.

**Must be right:** the deposit is what the excavation left in place, the same layered
sediment as the trench section.

**Deliberately wrong:** the deposit gives way. It is not walked on, and nothing in the
sources says any of it has ever collapsed under anybody. When the chapter is painted, the exit is a gap in the rock at the far end,
lit; the real visitor leaves by the door they came in, which is one of the level's lies.
