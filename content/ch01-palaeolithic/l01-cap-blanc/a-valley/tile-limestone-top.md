## What it is

The same ground with turf along the top rows: meadow green, a few grass blades over the top edge. The hiker's feet sit on the top edge.

## Where it stands in the game

Everywhere the valley floor meets the sky.

## Must be right

Tiles seamlessly with `tile-limestone` below and with itself sideways. The turf is the valley's, not lawn.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.

## Easter eggs

None.
