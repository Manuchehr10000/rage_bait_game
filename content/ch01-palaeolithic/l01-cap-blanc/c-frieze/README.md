# Beat c · the frieze

The shelter floor runs straight on to the back of the first horse: there is no gap to jump,
and nothing to fall into over the cast. Three horses under the museum's lamps, the same
sprite (`horse-relief.md`), 96 px apart, backs flush with the shelter floor.

Each horse is two floors: the **back**, and a step down, the **neck and head**, out to the
tip of the muzzle. The three teach the rhythm the level turns on: land on the back, run
out along the head, and jump from the tip of the muzzle. From the tip, a full jump lands on
the next back with its whole length to spare.

**The cast** (`skeleton-cast.md`) lies under the first horse, in a hollow of its own a tile
above the floor of the trench. It is drawn exactly, and nothing happens on it or near it:
the floor of the trench that kills begins past its hollow, the shelter floor runs straight
on to the first horse's back above it, and the first place anyone can come off the frieze,
the first horse's muzzle, is past it too. It is the one thing in the level that is a cast,
and it is labelled as one at the site.

A muzzle does not take a blow (see `../e-blow/README.md`). That is as true here as at the
fifth horse; a run that follows the rhythm never finds it out, and a man who jumps up and
down on a muzzle does. Identical things are identical (pillar 4).

Two bison in lower relief are on the wall above: lines, not ledges (`bison-relief.md`).
