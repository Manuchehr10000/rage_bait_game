# Beat e · the second blow

The fifth horse: the same sprite, **a head lower** than the other four (10 px), its back
beginning 22 px past the fourth's break, its muzzle whole. Close under the break, and low.

**The rule, for every horse.** A muzzle takes a man who walks out on to it, however long
he stands there, and not one who comes down on it: a landing on a muzzle from a fall of
more than 32 px (twice his height) knocks it off, and he goes down with it into the
trench. The thick of the head, the neck behind the break line, takes any blow; so nobody is
ever left on a neck with no muzzle and no way on. A man more than half over the muzzle when
it goes goes with it, all of him. No jump saves him once it has gone: there is nothing
left to push on.

**The trick: 'The second blow'.** The fourth horse has just taught him to jump from the
break. He does, the full jump he has made at every horse, and it carries him over the
fifth's back and down on its muzzle. It comes off, and leaves the same pale break the
fourth has. The first blow was the pick's, in 1909; this one is his. The fifth horse
claims the death (pillar 8), and so does any horse whose muzzle he knocks off.

**Once known:** a short jump from the break. Anything from a tap to a jump held about a
sixth of a second lands on the fifth's back; a full one lands on its muzzle. Then he walks
down on to the head, which holds, and jumps from the muzzle to the far floor as at the
first three.

It is the cows' shape (`content/tricks.md`): the lesson of the fourth is what kills at the
fifth. Its risk, accepted by the designer on 2026-09-28: the first time, it feels like the
ledge that takes no weight, Roc-aux-Sorciers' shadowless figure. It differs from the second
attempt on, when the muzzle holds a man who walks on to it.

**Must be right:** the fifth is the same horse as the other four in every pixel, only
lower. The muzzle that comes off falls to the floor of the trench, turning, and lies there.

**Deliberately wrong:** nobody has ever stood on the frieze, let alone broken it.
