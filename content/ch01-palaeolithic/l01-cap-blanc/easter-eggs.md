# Easter eggs · Cap Blanc

| Egg | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| The muzzle | Beat c, one horse | The tradition is that the discovering pick took the muzzle off a horse in 1909. If the painted frieze shows one horse with a damaged muzzle, it must be on all ten (pillar 4) or on none; so it goes on all ten, and the teacher will notice |
| Laussel | Beat a, the far layer | The Abri de Laussel, where the Venus of Laussel was found in 1911, is a few hundred metres along the same valley side. A second, smaller overhang in the far layer would be found by the right person |
| Commarque | Beat a | The keep across the valley is real and is drawn once, not repeated |
| Chicago | Beat c, the cast | The real skeleton is in the Field Museum. The cast is labelled as a cast at the site; the game's is a cast of a cast |
| The trench section | Beat c, the trench wall | The layers are the Magdalenian deposit; the excavators' section drawing is in Lalanne & Breuil 1911 |
