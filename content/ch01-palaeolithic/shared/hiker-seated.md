## What it is

The hiker sitting on the ground, knees up, hat on, lamp on, looking at nothing. The
pose for the one death you choose: pressing R, or leaving for the map.

## Where it stands in the game

Every level of chapter 1. Drawn with its bottom row on the floor where the tourist was
standing.

## Must be right

The same person and the same costume as the living frames. Sitting, not collapsed.
