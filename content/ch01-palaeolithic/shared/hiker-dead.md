## What it is

The hiker flat on their back, eyes shut, hat still on, headlamp still on. One frame. The
game rotates it to lie in a trench, tips it for a blow from a pick, sinks it for a
drowning, and would stretch or silhouette it for any later cause.

## Where it stands in the game

Every death in chapter 1 that leaves a body. Drawn where the living sprite was, so keep
the same 12 × 16 box and the same footprint.

## Must be right

- Must be the same person and the same costume as the living frames, one to one.
- The headlamp stays lit. That is the deadpan: the lamp does not know.
- No blood, no expression beyond deadpan. Pillar 8: a death looks like what caused it and
  nothing reacts.
- The outline must be a clean, readable shape on its own, because later chapters
  silhouette it.

## Deliberately wrong

None. Everything about this one is meant to be right.
