## What it is

The chapter's own brochure page, from Crete in the south to the Argolid in the north: the
north coast of Crete with Knossos and, across the island, Phaistos on the Messara; Thera and
the Cyclades; the Argolid with Mycenae and Tiryns. Sea, land, paper. No pins, no route, no
words. The game draws a pin for each site with a level, numbered like its bead on the ribbon,
and a single dotted leg from the previous site to the selected one. Sites with no level yet are
plain dots.

## Where it stands in the game

Shown after the player picks the Aegean on the world map, behind the pins, in the 208 × 152 map
box on the left of the screen.

## Must be right

- The coastlines of Crete, Thera and the Argolid. The game draws them from the survey now, so
  there is a true line to paint over.
- Thera drawn as it is now, a broken ring round the flooded caldera: the shape the eruption
  that buried Akrotiri left.
- The frame: ask for the exact longitude and latitude bounds before painting; they are computed
  from the five sites in `src/map/atlas.ts`.

## Deliberately wrong

Brochure scale and colour.

## Sources

Any pre-1930 Baedeker map of Greece; the Admiralty charts of the Aegean.

High.
