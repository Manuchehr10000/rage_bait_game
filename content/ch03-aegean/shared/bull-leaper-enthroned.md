## What it is

The one death in the game with no physical cause (pillar 8): landing in front of the throne at
Knossos seats him in it, and his visit is over. He sits in it facing out, hands on his knees,
feet on the floor, at rest. Drawn over the throne, centred on its footprint; the seat line is
row 10, the bottom row is the floor.

## Must be right

- Facing out, at rest. **Never triumphant and never a king**; never the slump of giving up
  (`bull-leaper-seated`). Its own frame, its own sound (a body sitting down on stone), its own
  label, "The throne". Nothing in the level reacts.
- No other level seats him in anything.

## Deliberately wrong

That it is a death at all.
