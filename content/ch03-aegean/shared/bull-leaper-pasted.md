## What it is

The snort's death in the Minotaur: blown up the hatch and pasted flat on the ceiling over it, face
up against it, splayed and still, in full colour, the wig over his eyes. The dead frame laid down,
head to the left, one arm hanging and one leg splayed. Row 0 is against the ceiling.

## Where it stands in the game

The Minotaur, beat d. Drawn at (48, 528) on T_end's ceiling over the hatch, x 48 to 66: his box
is at x 48 to 58, y 528 (`../l06-minotaur/LEVEL.md`, beat d), and the drawing reaches 8 px past
it, 2 px past the hatch, over the lip. (Settled when the deaths were drawn, 2026-10-10:
LEVEL.md's x 48 to 58 is his box, and the drawing stays 18 wide, so that he lies splayed, not
folded, across the whole of the hole.) Frames 9 to 44 of the snort's death. Before it, frames 0 to 8, he is his idle frame, unturned:
stiff where the sniff caught him for 5 frames, then carried straight up the hatch by the jet for
4. The dust is the beast's (`../l06-minotaur/c-way-down/beast-dust.md`).

## Must be right

- **Never turned, and the kilt never flies**: those are the horns' (`../l06-minotaur/LEVEL.md`,
  beat d). He is pasted, not tumbled.
- **Face up and splayed**, so that it reads as a man stuck to a ceiling and not a splat; the wig
  over his eyes, as in `bull-leaper-dead`.
- The same costume, the same person, in full colour: the only full colour on the screen.
- Nothing reacts.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The snort, all invented** (`../l06-minotaur/LEVEL.md`, Deliberately wrong): the jet and the
  ceiling.
