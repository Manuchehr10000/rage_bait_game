## What it is

Chapter 3's costume, from the research: "bull-leaper kit, badly tied and slipping". What a
costume shop sells as the youths of the Bull-Leaping Fresco: a black wig with one long lock, a
short red kilt with a blue edge and a gold-painted belt, knotted over his own T-shirt and
shorts, and his trainers. Four frames, strip left to right: idle, walk1, walk2, jump. Faces
right, drawn one pixel left of the 10 px hitbox, bottom edge on the floor.

**Provisional.** The costume has not had its own design conversation yet. This is the version
the level was built with; the details below are the ones any version has to keep.

## Where it stands in the game

Every level of chapter 3.

## Must be right

- Visibly fake and visibly his: the wig sits wrong, the belt is tied badly, the kilt hangs lower
  on one side, and the T-shirt shows. It mocks the tourist, never the fresco or the people in it
  (pillar 11).
- **No skin paint.** The fresco's figures are painted red and white by a convention of the art;
  the costume never imitates it.
- It never changes the hitbox (pillar 9).
- There is no bull anywhere in the chapter (designer's ruling). The costume is a bull-leaper's
  with nothing to leap.

## Deliberately wrong

All of it: it is a costume.
