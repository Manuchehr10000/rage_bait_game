## What it is

The idle frame with the wig knocked down over his eyes, sweatband and all, and his own sandy
hair showing on top. Used, stretched and rotated, by the chapter's deaths, but not by the throne,
which has its own frame (`bull-leaper-enthroned`), nor by the Minotaur's snort, clap and swat,
which have theirs (`bull-leaper-pasted`, `bull-leaper-clapped`, `bull-leaper-pressed`). In the
Minotaur it is turned only by whole quarters, never stretched or turned by less
(`../l06-minotaur/LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10): it is how the clothesline and the toss leave him,
flat on his back, after their drawn half-quarters (`bull-leaper-clotheslined`,
`bull-leaper-tumbling`). Corrected 2026-10-10: this said "every death but the throne".

## Must be right

- The same costume, the same person, the same own hair as `bull-leaper-seated`.
- The wig comes down; it never flies off, and nothing is made of it. A death looks like what
  caused it (pillar 8); the wig is only how he is lying.
