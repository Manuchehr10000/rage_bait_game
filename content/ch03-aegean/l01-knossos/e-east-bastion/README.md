# Beat e · the East Bastion

The way out, and honest. Along the corridor under the far hall, in the dark, and out onto
Evans's East Bastion terrace in full sun, over the Kairatos valley: three giant jars, the
turnstile, and the bastion's parapet at the end of the level, higher than any jump, so a jump
over the turnstile comes down on the terrace and he walks back to it. No jump here can kill.

The giant jar has a note here. The corridor's walls, the parapet's stone and the valley are
drawn by the game; the turnstile is the game's own exit, the same in every level.
