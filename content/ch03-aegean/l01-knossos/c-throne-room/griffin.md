## What it is

One of the pair of griffins painted either side of the throne, as the wall stands today: a
wingless, crested griffin couchant among reeds, on bands of red and off-white. The pair is the
Gilliérons' restoration.

## Where it stands in the game

On the Throne Room's wall either side of the throne, facing it. Drawn by the game in the dark.

## Must be right

- Drawn as the restored wall stands now, not as the Minoans painted it.
- Crested, wingless, couchant; reeds; the red ground.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The symmetry.** Galanakis, Tsitsa and Günkel-Maschek (BSA 2017) found no evidence for a
  griffin west of the throne: the scheme "as it currently stands, never existed". The level
  draws it as it stands, and this note says so.

## Sources

Galanakis, Tsitsa & Günkel-Maschek, "The Power of Images: Re-examining the Wall Paintings from
the Throne Room at Knossos", BSA 112 (2017).

## Confidence

The restored wall: solid. The Minoan original: disputed, and not what is drawn.
