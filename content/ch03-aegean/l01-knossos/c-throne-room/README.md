# Beat c · the Throne Room

In by the west door through the small rooms behind the throne, and down into the sunken basin,
where the room's only light falls. A full jump out of the basin, from anywhere in it, comes down
in front of the throne, and he is sitting in it. His visit is over. **Death label: "The
throne".** A hop out, short, and a walk past it: it is a chair. Over the barrier, a full jump
comes down in front of Evans's copy, the same chair to the pixel, and nothing happens. Four
steps up into the court.

This is the one death in the game with no physical cause (pillar 8). It has its own frame
(`../../shared/bull-leaper-enthroned.md`) and sound, and nothing reacts to it.

The throne and the griffin have notes here. The red walls, the benches, the basin's lining and
steps, the barrier post and the light are drawn by the game.
