## What it is

The bronze bust of Sir Arthur Evans on its tall plinth, which stands by the way in to the site.
Before any Minoan stone, the man who rebuilt it.

## Where it stands in the game

The West Court, between the spawn and the first pit. Its bottom edge is the court's paving.
Not solid. Faces right, into the level.

## Must be right

- A bronze head and shoulders on a tall, plain stone plinth.
- Evans as an older man: moustache, the set of his head. Look at the bust itself before painting.
- It is honoured, not mocked. The joke is the level's, never the bust's (pillar 11).

## Deliberately wrong

- The plinth is shortened to the level's scale.
- **No inscription.** No text inside a level (pillar 2).

## Sources

Photographs of the bust at the site entrance, for reference on your own screen only.

## Confidence

The bust and where it stands: solid. **Its date and its sculptor are not verified (1935 is
often said). Ask before anything depends on them.**
