# Beat a · the West Court

Before any Minoan stone, Evans's bronze bust by the way in; then the paved court with its
raised walkway, and three open round pits to hop or to fall into and climb out of. Between the
first two, one slab of the paving is over a fourth pit the dig never emptied, a narrow shaft:
it goes the frame he is on it and takes him to the bottom. **Death label: "The kouloura".** Three identical honest things open the level; the three catch-pits of beat g close it.

The pits are the *kouloures*: Old Palace pits that the Minoans filled and paved over when they
enlarged the court, emptied again by the dig. Two tiles wide and two deep in the game.

The paving and ashlar tiles and the bust have notes here. The slab that goes is a tile of
`tile-knossos-paving-top`, drawn by the code that draws the court, with the court's fill
under it until it goes. The pits' rubble linings and the walkway are drawn by the game.
