# Beat a · the West Court

Honest. Before any Minoan stone, Evans's bronze bust by the way in; then the paved court with
its raised walkway, and three open round pits to hop or to fall into and climb out of. Nothing
lies. Three identical honest things open the level; the three catch-pits of beat g close it.

The pits are the *kouloures*: Old Palace pits that the Minoans filled and paved over when they
enlarged the court, emptied again by the dig. Two tiles wide and two deep in the game.

The paving and ashlar tiles and the bust have notes here. The pits' rubble linings and the
walkway are drawn by the game.
