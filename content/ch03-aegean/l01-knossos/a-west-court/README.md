# Beat a · the West Court

Before any Minoan stone, Evans's bronze bust by the way in; then the paved court with its
raised walkway, and three open round pits to hop or to fall into and climb out of. Between the
first two, the court is a block over a fourth pit the dig never emptied, with no bottom: it
opens the frame he is on it and takes him down. **Death label: "The kouloura".** The way past is
down into the first pit and straight across into the second. The third pit's floor is a lid on
the same hole, and opens under whoever lands in it: it is hopped, or bounced off with a jump
pressed as he lands on it. One trick under one label: the court and the third pit's floor each
claim the fall down the hole they open (pillar 8), whatever finishes him in it.

The pits are the *kouloures*: Old Palace pits that the Minoans filled and paved over when they
enlarged the court, emptied again by the dig. Two tiles wide and two deep in the game.

Then up onto the stump of the west façade, and over it into the rooms behind the throne.

The paving and ashlar tiles and the bust have notes here. The block that goes is two tiles
of `tile-knossos-paving-top` on two of `tile-knossos-ashlar`, and the third pit's floor two of
`tile-knossos-ashlar-top`, drawn by the code that draws the court, with the court's fill under
them until they go. The pits' rubble linings and the walkway are drawn by the game.
