# Beat f · the Hall of the Double Axes

Out of the corridor into the west light well, the double axe cut at three heights in the blocks
of its wall, in the light. Then the dark hall and two doorways of folding doors on one clock, one
open at a time: the first folds away from him, the second, beside the light, toward him. A clack
each turn. Stand against the second the way the first taught him and it opens into his face.
**Death label: "The door".** Wait in the middle of the outer hall instead, never at a door, and
walk through into the sun. The first trap in the game to run on a cycle; it is the last in the
level (pillar 5).

Nothing here has a painted note yet: the leaves, the marks and the well's wall are drawn by the
game. Before any of them is painted, check which way each leaf folded and what the piers are
restored in (LEVEL.md, "Not verified").
