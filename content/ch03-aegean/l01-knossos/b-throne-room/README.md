# Beat b · the Throne Room

Over the stump of the west façade and down three steps through the small rooms behind the
throne, to the west door. The sunken basin starts at the last step, under the door, with the
room's only light falling into its eastern two tiles; a jump off the step meets the door, and one
taken over the edge is under the lintel, so the way on is down into the basin. A full jump out of
the light, or pressed as he drops in, comes down in front of the throne, and he is sitting in it. His visit is over. **Death label:
"The throne".** A hop out, held 6 to 8 frames, lands short, and a walk past it: it is a chair.
Over the barrier, a full jump comes down in front of Evans's copy, the same chair to the pixel,
and nothing happens. Four steps up into the court.

This is the one death in the game with no physical cause (pillar 8). It has its own frame
(`../../shared/bull-leaper-enthroned.md`) and sound, and nothing reacts to it. The court has just
taught him that the way out of a pit is a committed jump; here that jump is the one that kills,
and the hop lives.

The throne, the griffin and Evans's slab have notes here. The red walls, the benches, the
basin's lining and steps, the barrier post and the light are drawn by the game.
