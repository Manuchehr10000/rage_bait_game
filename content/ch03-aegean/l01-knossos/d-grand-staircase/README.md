# Beat d · the Grand Staircase

From the court into shade under Evans's upper storey, and down five steps, one storey, to the
upper hall, under a ceiling that cuts a jump short. Column A at its edge; then the light well,
three tiles of daylight to sunlit, drained paving one storey down; across it the far hall on
columns B and C, the same as A to the pixel. **Nothing here kills, and every column holds.** He
walks off into the light, falls a storey (75 px), lands in the sun and walks on east, in the
dark, along the corridor under the far hall and out onto the East Bastion's terrace; or he jumps
across onto the far hall, walks along it over the corridor, and off its end onto the terrace
(91 px). Both come out on the terrace.

It is the chapter's signature, shown honestly: storeys stacked on columns, five tiles floor to
floor, and the light well through all of them. One storey is nothing, and the light catches
every landing. Phaistos takes that away.

The column has a note here; the slab is beat b's. The far wall of the light well, its drain, the
daylight and the corridor's walls are drawn by the game.
