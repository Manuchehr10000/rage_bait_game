## What it is

`tile-knossos-slab` with sky on it: the flat concrete roof on top. Evans's roof over the west
storerooms VIII to XII, of 1929, and the rest of his roofs.

## Must be right

Bare grey concrete on top; wraps with the slab face below it.

## Deliberately wrong

As `tile-knossos-slab`. Whether the 1929 roof over the storerooms has beams or posts inside it
is not verified; the level puts his columns under it (see the level's deliberate lies).

## Sources

https://knossosdocumenta.gr/en/west-magazines/

## Confidence

The roof and its date: solid (confirmed against Knossos Documenta). Its inside: not verified.
