## What it is

A storage jar of the west storerooms: a big coarse clay jar for oil, wine or grain, as tall as a
man's shoulder, standing against the storeroom wall. Seven stand along the room.

## Where it stands in the game

Against the back wall of the storeroom, bottom edge on the floor. **Never solid**: a jar plus a
full jump would reach Evans's roof in the open stretches.

## Must be right

- A wide-shouldered clay jar with handles at the shoulder and a band of raised decoration.
- They stand in the storerooms today, where they were found or put back.

## Deliberately wrong

- One jar, repeated. Real ones differ in size and decoration.
- The jars stand along one storeroom; they are found across the row.

## Sources

Christakis, on the storage jars of the west wing, in *Knossos: Palace, City, State* (BSA
Studies 12, 2004). Kalokairinos opened six of these storerooms in 1878–79.

## Confidence

The jars in the storerooms: solid. Their decoration: generic, and marked so.
