## What it is

One of the giant storage jars of the Old Palace, taller than a man in reality: rope bands and
discs in relief round its body. They stand in the Magazines of the Giant Pithoi, beside the
East Bastion.

## Where it stands in the game

Three of them at the head of Evans's stair on the terrace, bottom edge on the terrace. Not solid.
Identical and honest.

## Must be right

- The rope bands and the rows of discs in relief.
- They are Old Palace jars.

## Deliberately wrong

- **They are moved to the head of the stair.** They stand in their own magazine beside it.
- Drawn about his height, shorter than they are.

## Sources

Evans, *The Palace of Minos* (the Magazines of the Giant Pithoi). The Ephorate's sign at the
East Bastion: https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=85506

## Confidence

The jars: solid. Their placing: the level's.
