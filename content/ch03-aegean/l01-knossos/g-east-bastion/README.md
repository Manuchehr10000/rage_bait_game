# Beat g · the East Bastion

The exit, and honest. The terrace in full sun, three giant jars at the head of the stair, and
Evans's stair down in two flights, the stone runnel beside it dropping step by step, with a
catch-pit of silt at the landing and at the foot. No jump here can kill. At the foot, the
turnstile, the same one that stood on a trapdoor at Karnak. This one is only a turnstile.

The giant jar has a note here. The runnel, its catch-pits and the valley are drawn by the game.
