# Theseus, paying out the thread

|---|---|
| Id | `theseus-payout` |
| File | None, and none is wanted: drawn in code at 1 world px (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, ruled 2026-10-10) |
| Size | 12 × 24 world px per frame, 4 frames in a horizontal strip, his box; faces right |
| Beat | `c-way-down` |

## What it is

Theseus crouched on his heels where he landed, paying out a loop of thread from the ball: his
head low (its top at row 9 of his box), his near arm going round. The four frames are the arm at
the four quarters of its circle: forward, down, back, up, his hand at (11, 16), (10, 18), (8, 17)
and (11, 13) of his box, where the ball is drawn. The loop itself is the thread's
(`../b-passage/thread.md`, item 7): a coil lying flat on the floor before him, 9 × 3 when whole,
its middle under his front column, laid from under his front foot out along the floor and round,
growing every frame after the first, never a ring stood up. (Drawn first, until the final
review, 2026-10-10, as a closed ring from his hand to the floor, up to 9 × 11: a white 0 or O
beside him.)

## Where it stands in the game

On L_A, L_B and L_C for 12 frames each, on J4 for 9 and on J3 for 6 (`../LEVEL.md`, beat c). The
arm goes twice round in each pay-out, so the frame changes every one or two frames. The loops are
left lying where he crouched, before his front foot. Behind the tourist, and behind him, as the
thread always is.

## Must be right

- **Drawn as work, never as waiting** (`../LEVEL.md`, beat c): the arm moves every frame, and
  the loop grows every frame after the first, on which it is laid under his front foot.
- **The loop lying on the floor, wider than tall**: never a closed ring stood up, which reads as
  the digit 0 or the letter O (pillar 2), and no straight line from his hand down to it, which
  with the ball and the floor reads as a T or an L.
- **A crouch, never a kneel**: the kneel is the door's (`../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`), and the
  two must not be one drawing.
- The arm 2 px, and the hand clear of his own body by a line of clay where it passes in front of
  it.
- As `../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`, "Theseus, in every pose".

## Deliberately wrong

- **The crouches paying out thread** (`../LEVEL.md`, Deliberately wrong): no source has him stop
  to pay it out.
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).

## Sources

Apollodorus, *Epitome* 1.9: "drawing it after him".
