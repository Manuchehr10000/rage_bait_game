# Beat c · the way down and the race

Honest: nothing here can kill. He walks off each corridor's end through five plain 16 px holes,
D0, D1, X2, D3 and X, and lands running the other way each time, down to T_end. From Z1 down he
hears a bull snore under the floor; from T he sees the hatch a storey below breathe, twin plumes
of dust rising 32 px on the out-breath and drawn back in. Beside him through the rock, Theseus
drops the four shafts onto their pillar tops and goes down Daedalus's four look-alike rooms,
crouching on each landing down to J3 to pay out a loop of thread, and wins the race into his
black doorway on row 5.

Notes here: Theseus falling and paying out, and the beast's dust, which beat d's trick turns on.
The holes are only the masonry's absence, and every one is the same plain hole, the hatch
included. The thread laid down his route is beat b's; the stone is beat a's.
