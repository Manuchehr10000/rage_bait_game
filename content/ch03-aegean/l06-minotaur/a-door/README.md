# Beat a · the door and the queue

He walks in off the left edge onto the outside ground, past the tribute waiting in one file at
the labyrinth's door, six youths and seven maidens, and past Ariadne, standing apart and facing
the door. Theseus kneels at the doorpost re-tying the thread in a fussy loop, eyes on the knot:
a solid box until the knot fires, the only solid person in the game. The tourist hops the
kneeling hero, who ignores him, and goes in first through the black vestibule. Nothing kills
here. It is the first bent figure he vaults, and the plant for the hands.

The first screen is the vase (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, ruled 2026-10-10): clay to the edges of the
screen and no sky, a heavier glaze ground line under the figures, and under it, in the strip of
ground outside the door, a band of black rays rising from below, as at an amphora's foot, so
that it reads as an amphora's panel: a tourist in full colour walking into a museum vase. The
file stands a figure every 6 px, each one a person, about eight on the screen with Ariadne and
the rest off its left edge. Since the air is clay outside and in, the labyrinth's outer face,
its lintel and the post tell the one from the other by the wall alone.

Notes here: the clay; the ground line and the rays under it; the outer face; the doorpost; the
vestibule; the masonry and its `-top`, which every beat uses; the youth and the maiden of the
file; Ariadne; Theseus kneeling, whose note is the one every other Theseus pose follows; and the
clay reserve round the tourist wherever glaze is behind him. (The band of tongues over the
outside, built first, was replaced by the rays the same day: `labyrinth-rays`.) The knot on the
post is the thread's (`../b-passage/thread.md`). Nothing in this level is painted: it is drawn
in code at 1 world px, and no note asks for a file.
