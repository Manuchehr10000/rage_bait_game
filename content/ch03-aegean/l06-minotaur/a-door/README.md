# Beat a · the door and the queue

He walks in off the left edge onto the outside ground, past the tribute waiting in one file at
the labyrinth's door, six youths and seven maidens, and past Ariadne, standing apart and facing
the door. Theseus kneels at the doorpost re-tying the thread in a fussy loop, eyes on the knot:
a solid box until the knot fires, the only solid person in the game. The tourist hops the
kneeling hero, who ignores him, and goes in first through the black vestibule. Nothing kills
here. It is the first bent figure he vaults, and the plant for the hands.

The first screen is the vase (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, ruled 2026-10-10): clay to the edges of the
screen and no sky, a band of black and red tongues over the outside, out of his reach, and a
glaze ground line under the figures, so that it reads as an amphora's panel: a tourist in full
colour walking into a museum vase. Since the air is clay outside and in, the labyrinth's outer
face, its lintel and the post tell the one from the other by the wall alone.

Notes here: the clay; the tongues; the ground line and the zone under it; the outer face; the
doorpost; the vestibule; the masonry and its `-top`, which every beat uses; the youth and the
maiden of the file; Ariadne; Theseus kneeling, whose note is the one every other Theseus pose
follows; and the clay reserve round the tourist wherever glaze is behind him. The knot on the
post is the thread's (`../b-passage/thread.md`). Nothing in this level is painted: it is drawn
in code at 1 world px, and no note asks for a file.
