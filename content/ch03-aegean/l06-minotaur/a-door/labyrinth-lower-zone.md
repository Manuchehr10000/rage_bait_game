# Under the ground line

|---|---|

| File | None, and none is wanted: drawn in code at 1 world px, by the masonry's rule's one variation (`tile-labyrinth`) |

## What it is

The band under the panel's ground line outside, as a vase has a band under its figure panel:
the masonry's wash, plain, with no joints, from the ground line (`labyrinth-ground-line`) down
to its course line at y 191, which runs on across the level. On its right, at x 80, the outer
face goes on down beside it from y 163 to that line (`labyrinth-face`), so the band ends where
the labyrinth begins. Under the course line, the labyrinth's masonry by its rule.

## Where it stands in the game

The bottom of the first screen, under the queue and the ground line (the camera's bottom is at
y 233 at the spawn), x 0 to 79. Z1, the first corridor down, runs under it from x 64 at y 208.

## Must be right

- **Distinct from the labyrinth's masonry** (2026-10-10): plain wash, no joints, closed by the
  outer face and the course line, so the outside reads as the vase's panel over its band and the
  labyrinth as built stone beside and under it, never as castle brick to the screen's edge.
- **Wash, never glaze.** A black band under the panel would be glaze, and in this level glaze is
  a person or the dark (the vestibule, the hero's doorway); a black floor would read as a hole.
- **No earth, turf, grass, rays or landscape.** A vase panel has no landscape, rays would read
  as spikes, and the labyrinth is plain masonry only (`../LEVEL.md`, Deliberately wrong).
- No lit top (`tile-labyrinth`), and no line under the figures' feet but the ground line's.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The vase's band under its panel**, drawn in the masonry's dilute wash and not in full glaze,
  as the ground outside a building.
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10), as everywhere in the level.

## Sources

The amphora's layout, at search-extract level: under the figure panel, a black band and rays
above the foot (the research, 2026-10-10).

## Confidence

Design choice. The layout it stands for is at search-extract level, and no vase was looked at.
This note first had the labyrinth's own masonry under the ground line, joints and all; it was
made a plain band when the world was drawn (2026-10-10), for the stage's direction that the
outside's lower zone be distinct from the masonry.
