# Under the ground line

## What it is

What lies under the ground line outside: the labyrinth's own masonry, by its rule. The labyrinth
runs on under the ground there (Z1, the first corridor down, reaches x 64 under the outside at
y 208), so the stone under the queue is its stone. On the vase this is where the glazed lower
body runs under a panel, and the masonry's dilute-glaze wash stands for it.

## Where it stands in the game

The bottom of the first screen, under the queue and the ground line, down to Z1's roof at y 208
(the camera's bottom is at y 233 at the spawn). It is listed so that nothing else is ever drawn
there.

## Must be right

- **The masonry, identical by construction**: its courses and joints run across x 80 into the
  stone under the door and the passage without a seam.
- **Wash, never glaze.** A black band under the panel would be glaze, and in this level glaze is
  a person or the dark (the vestibule, the hero's doorway); a black floor would read as a hole.
- **No earth, turf, grass, rays or landscape.** A vase panel has no landscape, and the labyrinth
  is plain masonry only (`../LEVEL.md`, Deliberately wrong).
- No lit top (`tile-labyrinth`).

## Deliberately wrong

- **Everything under the ground is the labyrinth's masonry**, and the vase's glazed lower body is
  shown by the masonry's wash, not by full glaze.
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10), as everywhere in the level.

## Sources

The amphora's layout, at search-extract level: under the figure panel, a black band and rays
above the foot (the research, 2026-10-10).

## Confidence

Design choice. The layout it stands for is at search-extract level, and no vase was looked at.
