## What it is

The jamb of the door, in the thickness of the outer wall: 3 px of the masonry's wash, x 80 to
82, edged with 1 px of glaze at x 83 where it meets the door opening, from the lintel at y 80 to
the floor at 160. Theseus ties the thread to it: the knot (`../b-passage/thread.md`) sits on its
wash at x 80 to 81, y 148 to 150.

## Where it stands in the game

At the door, x 80, from the first frame to the last. Theseus kneels against it, his box x 66 to
78; the knot fires the line from it; the tableau's Theseus stops at it with the body.

## Must be right

- **The thread is tied at the door** (Apollodorus, *Epitome* 1.9: "Theseus fastened it to the
  door").
- **The kneeling hero's hand reaches x 81 at most**, onto the wash, a pixel short of the glaze
  edge: glaze never touches glaze (`theseus-kneel`).
- **In the closing tableau its foot is crossed**, by Theseus's far arm reaching back to the horn
  at about y 146 to 149 and by the dead head's muzzle over x 82 and 83. Both are in front of it,
  in their reserved outline: there its glaze edge gives way to that 1 px line of clay, and the
  line is cut into its wash (`../f-thread/theseus-drag.md`, `../f-thread/minotaur-dead.md`). The
  knot stays over the arm.
- Stone, not a person: the wash, never full glaze over its width.
- Plain: no carving, no ring, no mark.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The knot on the doorpost**, where *Epitome* 1.9 says only "the door" (`../LEVEL.md`,
  Deliberately wrong).
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).

## Sources

Apollodorus, *Epitome* 1.9 (tr. J. G. Frazer, 1921), as `../LEVEL.md` reads it.

## Confidence

The thread fastened to the door: solid at search-extract level. **Not verified:** Frazer's note
that the thread was tied to the lintel.
