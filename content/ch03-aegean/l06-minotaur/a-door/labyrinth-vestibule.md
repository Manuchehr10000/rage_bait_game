## What it is

The dark room behind the door, from the door's head at y 80 to the floor at 160: a rectangle of
full glaze laid over the clay. It is air: he walks through it, the hero walks through it, the
knot's line crosses it, and the closing tableau comes out of it.

## Where it stands in the game

Between the door opening (x 80 to 96) and the passage (from x 128), on the first screen and the
last.

## Must be right

- **One of the two places where black glaze is the dark**, with the hero's doorway (`../LEVEL.md`,
  Art).
- **Whatever passes in front of it is cut from it by reserved clay**, so nothing is lost in it:
  - the tourist, by his reserve (`tourist-reserve`);
  - Theseus, walking through it after the knot, by a reserved line round him: the clay-gap rule's
    one exception is the hero's doorway, not this room;
  - the tableau's Theseus and the dead body, in a reserved outline (`../f-thread/minotaur-dead.md`).
- The knot's line crosses it in white at y 149, its best read (white on glaze is 18 to 1).
- Nothing else in it: no door leaf, no steps, no wall line.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The vase's glaze standing for darkness**; the vestibule itself is the game's.
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).
