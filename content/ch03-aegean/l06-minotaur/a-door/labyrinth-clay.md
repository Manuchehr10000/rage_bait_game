# The clay

## What it is

The ground of the vase: the orange clay a black-figure painter leaves bare. In the level it is
the air, everywhere: outside the door, over the labyrinth's roof, and in every corridor, room,
shaft and the cell. It is also what every reserved line in the level is: an eye, an incision, the
gap between two glaze shapes. One flat colour, `#c8743d`, the same everywhere.

## Where it stands in the game

Behind everything, from x 0 to 320 and y 0 to 752. It replaces the warm sky the rough build drew
outside the door and over the roof, which was fixed to the screen and did not move with the
camera. The vestibule and the hero's doorway are glaze laid over it. Its top 14 rows are never on
the screen: the camera stops there to keep the vase's foot whole (`labyrinth-rays`,
2026-10-10), two rows of clay over the roof's line.

## Must be right

- **Clay to the edges of the screen, and no sky** (ruled 2026-10-10). No gradient, no light, no
  texture, no grain: flat, as the vase's ground is.
- **One clay, outside and in.** The wall alone tells the outside from the inside
  (`labyrinth-face`).
- **Air is clay, and only air and reserved lines are.** Stone is the masonry's wash, people are
  glaze or cream, the dark is glaze, the tourist is in full colour and the thread is white. A
  reserved line is this clay and never a lighter or darker one: an incision is the clay showing
  through; in the ground outside, the two rows over and under the ground line's glaze are
  (`labyrinth-ground-line`). The band of rays under it stands on the ground's stone, never on
  clay (designer, on a recommendation, 2026-10-11): this note's exception for that strip, the
  clay of the vase's ornament between its rays in solid ground, read as a pit of spikes under a
  ledge, and is superseded.
- Never the thread's white and never the women's cream.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The level is the vase** (ruled 2026-10-10): the clay stands for the air, outside and inside a
  building, which no vase shows. Outside the door there is no sky though he is under it.
- **Black-figure for a section view** (`../LEVEL.md`, Deliberately wrong): the vases never draw
  the labyrinth in section.
- **Drawn, not painted.** Flat, unlit pixel art in code at 1 world px: the level is exempt from
  the painted style of `content/README.md` (soft interiors, light from the upper right), which
  black-figure is not (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).

## Sources

- The technique: the figures are painted in a slip that fires black on the clay-coloured ground,
  and incised through the slip to the clay (Metropolitan Museum, Heilbrunn Timeline, "Athenian
  Vase Painting: Black- and Red-Figure Techniques":
  https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/athenian-vase-painting-black-and-red-figure-techniques).

## Confidence

The technique: at search-extract level (the research, 2026-10-10). No vase was looked at
(designer's ruling, 2026-10-10: drawn from the research, without images). The colour is the
game's choice of an Attic clay orange, measured from no vase.
