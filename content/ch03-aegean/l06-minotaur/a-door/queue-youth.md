## What it is

One of the six Athenian youths sent with seven maidens as the Minotaur's tribute, waiting in the
file at the labyrinth's door. A beardless young man in a short chiton, all in glaze, standing
still and facing the door, his hands empty at his sides. His eye a dot of reserved clay, high and
forward in the head; a fillet of added red round his head over the glaze. He is 24 px tall, as
all the story's people are, against the tourist's 16 (ruled 2026-10-10: they stay taller).

As drawn (2026-10-10, when the file was opened to a figure every 6 px), a person whole in
silhouette, about 6 px wide:

- **His head**, rows 0 to 4: round, 3 px at the crown and 6 at its widest, his nose a pixel
  forward of his brow and his chin under it. The fillet across it on row 1, three pixels of red
  between glaze; his eye, the clay pixel under the fillet's front end, glaze either side of it
  and under it.
- **His neck**, row 5, 2 px, a lock of his hair hanging behind it with clay between.
- **His body**, the short chiton from his shoulders on row 6 to its hem on row 15, solid glaze:
  no incision down it. His near hand is a pixel forward at his thigh, on row 13; the hem flares a
  pixel behind him.
- **His legs**, rows 16 to 23, 2 px each with a pixel of clay between, and his feet on row 23,
  the front one a pixel forward.

## Where it stands in the game

In the file of thirteen outside the door, on the outside floor (y 136 to 160), on the ground
line: maidens and youths alternately, a maiden at the front and one at the back, one figure every
6 px (ruled 2026-10-10). Each figure is drawn over the one ahead of it and cut from it by a
reserved line of clay down its own front, and over and under it, corners included, so that every
face is whole and the one ahead loses only a pixel of its back. The front figure's box ends at
x 41, its face at x 39; the seventh's box begins at x −5, her face whole at the screen's left
edge; the rest of the file stands off the screen. Nothing of the file is at x 40 or more. They
stand still from the first frame to the last, and are still waiting when he leaves.

At the spawn three youths are on the screen, their eyes at x 7, 19 and 31 on y 138, between the
maidens' at x 1, 13, 25 and 37 on y 140 (`tests/minotaur-out.spec.ts`). Every youth stands
behind a maiden and in front of one: the maiden behind him takes the pixel of his back, x 3 of
his box, with her reserved line, and he takes hers. The count of thirteen is given up (ruled
2026-10-10).

## Must be right

- **A person**, whole enough to read as one at a figure every 6 px (ruled 2026-10-10): his head,
  neck, shoulders, body and two legs, with clay round them. The youths' black heads, with their
  clay eyes and red fillets, alternate with the maidens' cream faces, and are a pixel higher.
- **No letter or digit** (pillar 2). Drawn first with a reserved line down his body for his arm,
  he read with the gap between his legs as a letter A when the file was opened; the line went.
- **Six youths and seven maidens, besides Theseus**: he went as one of the seven youths, and
  Aegeus cast the lots for the rest (Plutarch, *Theseus* 17).
- **Never Theseus**: no sword, no scabbard, nothing in his hands. Theseus has the sword and the
  scabbard and no fillet (`theseus-kneel`).
- Beardless: young men are beardless in black-figure (the research).
- Profile, facing the door; still; every youth identical to the pixel.
- Clay between every two glaze shapes, corners included (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10): the cut
  down each figure's front, over and under it and round its corners, and a clay pixel between
  his feet and the ground line's glaze.
- Added red only on the fillet, over glaze.
- Never a hooded monk: the rough's all-glaze youths read as one. The head, its eye and its fillet
  are what make him a youth.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The queue** at the labyrinth's door (designer, 2026-10-07): the vases put youths at the fight,
  as witnesses (the research: Getty 86.AE.60, Met 56.171.12, the Tampa hydria, Met 09.221.39).
  Drawn as one vase file.
- **The step**, 6 px, a person each, about eight on the screen with Ariadne and the rest off its
  left edge (ruled 2026-10-10). Drawn first a figure every 3 px, each showing about 3 px of
  itself so that all thirteen heads could be counted, the file read as a comb, not people.
- **The red fillet**, to tell the youths from Theseus.
- **One youth, six times.**
- **Drawn, not painted.** Flat, unlit pixel art in code at 1 world px: the level is exempt from the
  painted style of `content/README.md` (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).
