## What it is

Theseus on his left knee at the doorpost, his right foot forward, re-tying Ariadne's thread in a
fussy loop with his eyes on the knot. The frames:

0. Wrap: his hand over the top of the post, at the knot.
1. Wrap: his hand round under it.
2. Tug: his hand pulled back toward his chest.
3. Pick: his fingers at the knot.

The loop is 40 frames long and never stops: frame 0 for 8, 1 for 8, 2 for 8 and 3 for 16, and
round again (the rough build's 16 of wrapping, 8 of tugging and 16 of picking).

## Theseus, in every pose

This note is the figure every other Theseus pose is drawn from: `../b-passage/theseus-stand.md`,
`-walk`, `-lean` and `-climb`; `../c-way-down/theseus-fall.md` and `-payout`;
`../e-cell/theseus-leap.md`, `-grip`, `-duck`, `-draw` and `-blow`; `../f-thread/theseus-drag.md`.

- **A beardless youth in a short chiton**, belted, to mid-thigh; arms and legs bare; all in
  glaze. Standing he is 24 px tall in a 12 × 24 box: the story's people stay taller than the
  tourist (ruled 2026-10-10).
- **In profile.** His eye a dot of reserved clay, high and forward and large, as an archaic eye
  is; his hair short, in glaze. No beard, no helmet, no cloak.
- **His sword in its scabbard** at his left hip on a baldric, the scabbard behind him as he faces
  right; in the fight the sword is in his right hand. The baldric is a 1 px reserved line across
  his chest. The blade is a 1 px glaze line, and a line of reserved clay wherever it lies over
  glaze.
- **No added red on him anywhere**, so that he is the same inside the labyrinth and out, and red
  never comes near a blow.
- **Two or three incisions a pose**, which separate and never model: the near arm from the body
  where it crosses it, the belt or the chiton's hem.
- **Limbs at least 2 px.** 1 px only for the sword, the incisions and the thread, which is the
  thread's (`../b-passage/thread.md`), in his hand or not.
- **He turns by a flip**, and looks back with his head alone (`../b-passage/theseus-walk.md`).
- **He never looks at the tourist** (`../LEVEL.md`, beat a).
- **Told from the six youths** by the sword and scabbard, and the red fillet he does not wear;
  **from the Minotaur** by a man's head.
- **Behind the tourist**, except while he mantles up O1 (frames 66 to 85 of his route). Cut by a
  reserved line of clay from any glaze he is in front of or behind (`../LEVEL.md`, Art,
  2026-10-10), except in his black doorway, where he is unseen until he steps out.

## Where it stands in the game

At the post from the first frame of every attempt until the knot fires, when the tourist's centre
reaches x 144. His body fills the solid box, x 66 to 78 and y 146 to 160: he is the only solid
person in the game, and a tourist who jumps onto him stands on his drawn back at y 146. Only his
arm leaves the box, to the knot at x 80 to 81, and it reaches x 81 at most: the post's wash, a
pixel short of its glaze edge at x 83. His knee and foot rest on y 159, over the ground line's
clay.

## Must be right

- **Busy hands, seen.** He is the first bent figure the tourist vaults, and the plant for the
  hands (`../LEVEL.md`, beat a): a bent man whose hands are busy. The arm 2 px, the hand clear
  against the post's wash, each of the four frames distinct in silhouette.
- **Eyes on the knot**, his head bent to it. He never looks at the man who vaults him.
- **Fussy, never waiting**: the loop is always moving.
- **The solid box is what is drawn**: nothing of his body outside x 66 to 78 or above y 146.
- **The thread tied at the door** (Apollodorus, *Epitome* 1.9).
- **The seventh youth**, by his own offer (Plutarch, *Theseus* 17): his dress is a youth's, with
  the sword.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The re-tie loop, kneeling, at the doorpost** (accepted by the designer, 2026-10-07 and 08);
  the knot on the post, where *Epitome* 1.9 says only "the door".
- **Theseus the only solid person** in the game, and only while he kneels.
- **The sword**, where *Epitome* 1.9 has his fists: the vases' sword, chosen by the designer
  (2026-10-06).
- **The youths' red fillets, which he does not wear**: the game's way to tell him from them.
- **Drawn, not painted.** Flat, unlit pixel art in code at 1 world px: the level is exempt from the
  painted style of `content/README.md` (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).

## Confidence

His dress: at search-extract level, from one record of fragments. No vase was looked at
(designer's ruling, 2026-10-10). **Not verified:** how he wears the scabbard (on a baldric or at
the belt) and on which side; his hair; whether the vases' Theseus wears anything on his head. No
source gives him kneeling at the door.
