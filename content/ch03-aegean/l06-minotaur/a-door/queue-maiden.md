## What it is

One of the seven Athenian maidens of the tribute, waiting in the file at the door. A peplos to
her feet in glaze, flaring a little at the hem; her hair in glaze, bound up behind; her face, her
near arm at her side and her feet in cream, `#f1dfb9`, each inside a 1 px line of the glaze; her
eye a dot of glaze in the cream face. Standing still, facing the door, hands empty.

## Where it stands in the game

In the file of thirteen outside the door, alternating with the youths, a maiden at the front and
one at the back (`queue-youth`, for the file's step, overlap and order). Four are on the screen
at the spawn, their eyes at x 1, 13, 25 and 37 on y 140. As drawn (2026-10-10), at a figure every
6 px each maiden is seen whole but for the back of her hair and a pixel of her back, which the
youth behind her takes with his reserved line: her face, three rows of cream with her eye, a dot
of glaze, in the middle of it, the glaze of her profile in front, and her hair over it; her cream
arm down her peplos; her cream feet. The fourth, at the screen's left edge, shows her face whole
and the front of her body. (At a figure every 3 px, first, only her face and arm were seen.)

## Must be right

- **Women's flesh in cream, never the thread's white** (designer, 2026-10-08). Cream as the vases'
  added white, laid over the glaze: a line of glaze round it, which it needs, since cream on the
  clay is only 2.66 to 1 (the research).
- **Her face whole**, and the woman whole enough to read as a person at a figure every 6 px
  (ruled 2026-10-10): a cream face in the file's line of heads, a pixel lower than the youths'.
- No added red on her: Ariadne's red is what tells her apart.
- Every maiden identical to the pixel.
- Clay between every two glaze shapes, corners included (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).
- The cream is the one tests pin, `241, 223, 185` (`tests/minotaur-out.spec.ts`); it stays.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The queue** at the door (designer, 2026-10-07), as `queue-youth`.
- **One maiden, seven times.**
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).
