# Beat f · out by the thread

Honest, and never replayed: the way of winning. Along row 5 and up Daedalus's four rooms, where
the thread goes up one of each room's two identical ceiling holes and the other is a closed
niche; up the column by the ledges beside the shafts and the pillar tops the hero landed on;
along G0, down O1 into the passage, past the knot still on the post and out at the door he came
in by. As his left edge passes x 80 the closing tableau begins, a staged second copy: Theseus
comes out of the black vestibule edge by edge dragging the dead Minotaur by a horn and stops at
the post where he knelt, the head and horns across the threshold, the rest unseen in the
vestibule's black, all drawn behind the tourist. (Until 2026-10-11 the rest was drawn in the
vestibule in a reserved outline; nothing inside the black is outlined now.) He leaves first, the queue still waits, and Ariadne looks
past him.
