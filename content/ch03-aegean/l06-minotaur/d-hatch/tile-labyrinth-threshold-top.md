## What it is

The dressed threshold before the hatch, the only fine stone in T_end's floor: one smooth block,
not coursed, in the dressed stone's paler tone (`#a8693e`), edged in a 1 px glaze contour. Its
top is worn by two foot hollows where the tribute stood:

- Its top contour is level all along it, x 64 to 79 on y 576, the floor's top row, which his
  feet stand on, as they stand on every block's top: no dish, notch, step or point in it.
- Under it two foot hollows, 4 px each, x 67 to 70 and 73 to 76, 2 px of the dressed stone
  between them: the stone under the contour there, on y 577, is worn paler (`#c48552`), one row
  and never a crescent.

Its edge at the hatch is cut square, the contour down x 64; along its underside, over the cell,
the contour is where the stone ends. Its right side is the plain block's joint at x 80, as every
block's is. (Redrawn 2026-10-11, on the designer's ruling that no shape reads as a letter, pillar
2: each hollow was 5 px wide and 2 deep, at 2 and 9 px from its left, the contour dipping into
each with a paler crescent under it, and the edge's two dips and three teeth read as a W or a
crown. Redrawn first the same day as one shallow dish, x 66 to 77 worn a pixel down, its
contour on y 577 and clay over it on y 576, with the hollows paler under it on y 578; but the
stone is solid from y 576, so a man standing in the dish stood a row of clay over its contour
across 12 of its 16 px, which no note had chosen, after a check of the ground stage. Both
readings are superseded.)

## Where it stands in the game

T_end's floor, x 64 to 80, between the hatch (x 48 to 64) and the plain blocks. A step on it, his
box over x 64 to 80, rings, and brings the beast back at once. One puff of the clock rises at it
(`../c-way-down/beast-dust.md`). The tourist leaps from the plain block before it and never
touches it.

## Must be right

- **It reads as a stone, and a different one.** The snort's answer is to leap it, so he must see
  it as stone. In the rough it was 1.27 to 1 on the clay and its hollows were clay pixels that read
  as air, a second hole: the glaze contour, round it and level along its top, is what makes it
  stone.
- **The one dressed stone** (`../LEVEL.md`, beat d): the plain blocks after it are pixel-identical
  to one another, and it is not them (`tests/minotaur-snort.spec.ts`).
- **Its worn hollows are the one wear in the level**, where the tribute stood before it went in.
- **No part of it reads as a letter or digit** (pillar 2; designer, 2026-10-11): the top's
  contour is level, never notched by dips with teeth between them, which read as a W or a
  crown; the hollows are paler stone under it, a row deep, never crescents, which side by side
  would make a w, and never darker than the stone, which as two dashes would read as minus
  signs. Checked at native size and on the screen canvas at window scales 3 and 5, with him
  standing on it and without, and pinned pixel for pixel (`tests/minotaur-snort.spec.ts`).
- **No crack**: in this game a crack means "will give", and this stone never gives.
- **His feet on its contour.** Standing on it, his soles are on y 575 and its top contour on
  y 576 is directly under them the whole stone along, as the floor's top row is under them on
  every block: never a row of clay between, which would stand him in the air over it. The
  tourist's feet are not glaze, so the contour may meet them.
