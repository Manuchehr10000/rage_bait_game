## What it is

The dressed stone of `tile-labyrinth-threshold-top` with stone over it. The game asks every level
for a buried tile and a `-top`, but the level has one dressed stone, the lip, and it always has
air over it: this tile is never drawn.

## Where it stands in the game

Nowhere. If a dressed stone were ever buried, it would be the lip's stone with no hollows and no
contour along its top.

## Must be right

- Never drawn in this level. A second dressed stone would be a second lip, and there is one.
