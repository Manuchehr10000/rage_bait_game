## What it is

Theseus up off his knee at the post, testing his knot: both hands on the line just inside the
post at (81, 149), his feet planted in his box, x 66 to 78, leaning back away from the post. The
frames, counted from the yank (the line taut at 0):

0. Upright, his hands on the line, from −16 to −11.
1. Half leaned, his head 3 px back, from −10 to −5.
2. Full lean, his head 6 px back and his front leg braced straight, from −4 through the 22
   frames he holds the line taut, to 21.

Before −16 he is still kneeling (`../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`); at 22 he lets go and walks in.

## Where it stands in the game

At the post, x 60 to 82, from the moment the tourist's centre reaches x 144 until he lets the
line go. The creak is the level's sound, not this drawing's.

## Must be right

- **The tell the knot's second attempt is timed against, read in silhouette.** The rough leaned
  3 px over 24 rows and could barely be seen. A full 6 px at the head and a braced leg, on the
  same frames, so that no window changes: the line's kill and its windows by hold are pinned
  (`tests/minotaur-theseus.spec.ts`).
- **Eyes on his knot**, never on the tourist.
- His hands on the line at the post: the line runs from his hands and the knot along the passage.
- As `../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`, "Theseus, in every pose".

## Deliberately wrong

- **The knot test** (`../LEVEL.md`, Deliberately wrong): the lean, the line rising to a hero's shin
  height and the tourist's throat.
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).
