## What it is

Theseus at his pace, 3 px a frame (180 px/s), the figure of `../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`. The
frames:

0. A stride, his legs apart.
1. The other half of it, his legs together.
2. Frame 0 with his head turned back over his shoulder.
3. Frame 1 with his head turned back.

The stride changes every 9 px he goes, every 3 frames.

## Where it stands in the game

- Along the passage after the knot, from the post to O1, looking back at his knot for his first
  10 frames (frames 2 and 3), and through the vestibule, cut from its black by a reserved line.
- Along G0 overhead, heard; along each pillar top and room to the next way down; along row 5 and
  into his doorway.
- In the fight, out to the edge of row 5 after he steps out.

While he carries the ball, the thread's 2 × 2 of white is in his hand (`thread`).

## Must be right

- **The look-back is his head alone.** Flipping the whole figure would have him walking backwards.
- **A hero's pace**, never a run that reads as fleeing or chasing.
- He never looks at the tourist: looking back, he looks at his knot.
- As `../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`, "Theseus, in every pose".

## Deliberately wrong

- **The hero's pace** (`../LEVEL.md`, Deliberately wrong): 180 px/s and a route that always
  arrives first.
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).
