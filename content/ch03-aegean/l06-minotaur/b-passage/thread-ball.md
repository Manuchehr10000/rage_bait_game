## What it is

The ball the thread is wound on: plain white, `#ffffff`, its corners cut so it is round, with no
mark on it. The line leaves it at its top left.

## Where it stands in the game

Wedged on the passage floor at the line's inner end, x 208 to 214 and y 154 to 160, under O1,
until Theseus takes it at the foot of his climb; in his hand, 2 × 2, while he carries it, but
never in the black (`thread`, item 8). From the fight on, on the floor of his black doorway on
row 5, where he left it, wholly in its black, and so never drawn: the thread leads into the dark
to it (`../e-cell/hero-doorway.md`). (Until 2026-10-11 it was drawn there once he had stepped
out, white in the black at his heels; nothing inside the black is drawn now but the thread.)

## Must be right

- **Plain.** The rough's two stripes read as a face, or a marshmallow.
- **The thread's white**, the only white: it is the thread.
- **Nothing on the wall over it.** It was kept clear of the knob over it, 10 px of clay between,
  until the stride's stone boss was dropped (designer, on a recommendation, 2026-10-11); the
  wall at O1's mouth is now plain clay.
- Nothing in the doorway's black, ever, but the thread: Theseus waits there unseen, and the ball
  left there is unseen too.

## Deliberately wrong

- **The ball wedged at the line's inner end**, where the knot test needs it (`../LEVEL.md`,
  Deliberately wrong), and left in his doorway.
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).

## Sources

Apollodorus, *Epitome* 1.9 (tr. J. G. Frazer): "by drawing the clue after him"; a clue is a ball
of thread.

## Confidence

The clue: solid at search-extract level. Where it lies is a design choice.
