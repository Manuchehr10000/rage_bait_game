## What it is

Theseus leaping low over the crouched bull from row 5's edge to the floor before its face: leaning
into it, his front knee up and his back leg trailing, his sword up over his head in his right
hand. The figure of `../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`.

## Where it stands in the game

From L+4, out of his box at x 140 on row 5, about 6 px of rise, down to x 94 on the cell floor.
Before it he walks out of his doorway (`../b-passage/theseus-walk.md`); after it he turns to the
bull (`../b-passage/theseus-stand.md`) and takes the horn at the grip.

## Must be right

- **The vases' warrior, never a bull-leaper** (`../LEVEL.md`): no vault, no handspring, no hands
  on the bull. He is never the Bull-Leaping fresco's figure, nor Renault's bull-dancer.
- **Low**, about 6 px of rise: the tourist's own leap after his is the hands' answer, and his must
  read as the one to follow.
- The sword 1 px, held up clear of the bull, a line of clay where it crosses any glaze.
- Cut from the bull's glaze by reserved clay as he passes over it.
- As `../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`, "Theseus, in every pose".

## Deliberately wrong

- **Theseus leaping low over the bull onto the horn**, in the vases' dress with the sword: no vase
  shows it (`../LEVEL.md`, Deliberately wrong).
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).
