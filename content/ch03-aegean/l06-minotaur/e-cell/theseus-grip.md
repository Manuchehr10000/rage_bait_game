## What it is

Theseus braced at the bull's head, his feet apart, the horn in his left hand, his sword held up in
his right, its blade upright along his body. His left arm, the far one, is not in this frame: the
code draws it, 2 px of glaze from his far shoulder to the horn, with a 2 × 2 hand on it, wherever
the horn is. The figure of `../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`. The frames:

0. Braced: his feet apart, his right fist before his belt, the blade upright from it in front of
   his chest and face, 6 px, a pixel of clay from both, its pommel under his fist. The guard he
   stands at on row 5 and in the cell is this frame without the horn (`../b-passage/theseus-stand.md`).
1. Shoved: the same, leaning into it, his hips back and his back leg braced straight behind him,
   while the struck body's lurch carries him on the horn to the left wall and back. Nothing of it
   in his box's first column, so that the clay round him never notches the wall he is shoved
   against.

**The horn while it is in his reach** (drawn 2026-10-10): his far hand has it while it is within
14 px of his far shoulder, a straight arm. The heave lifts it out of reach: he lets go of it to duck
(`theseus-duck`), and takes it again as he draws back for the first blow, at L+70, when it has
come down; the second blow jerks the head up out of his hand. So the horn is in his hand from L+40
to 57, and from L+70 to 129 (`tests/minotaur-bull.spec.ts`). (The notes first had it in his hand
through the heave: drawn, his arm stretched to the risen horn was 20 px, twice an arm.)

## Where it stands in the game

At the horn, his box x 94 to 106 before its face, from the grip at L+40, between the duck and the
blows, and on the horn as the struck body lurches, carried with it to the left wall and back.

In every pose of the fight he stands between the bull's body and its near arm: in front of its
body, its head and its far arm, cut from them by his reserved line, corners included, and never
into the floor he stands on; behind its near arm, the hand on it and the near stone, which are
cut from him by theirs (`minotaur-body`, Against Theseus). His arm and hand on the horn are in
front of all of it. (After a check of the drawn bull, 2026-10-10: his line went only left, right,
up and down, and the bull's glaze met his at the corners.) In his two blows he lunges in past its
near arm, and all of him is in front of it (`theseus-blow`).

## Must be right

- **The horn in his left hand** (`../LEVEL.md`, Must be right), as on the British Museum's
  neck-amphora 1843,1103.21 (the research). On MFA 60.1 he grips it with one hand, which hand not
  verified.
- **The sword's blade a line of reserved clay** where it lies over his own glaze body.
- **His far arm 2 px**, never the rough's 1 px line, and cut by clay where it crosses the bull.
- Clay between him and the bull everywhere they meet, corners included
  (`tests/minotaur-bull.spec.ts`).
- As `../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`, "Theseus, in every pose".

## Deliberately wrong

- **Held at the horn through the fight**, but while the heave has lifted it out of his reach, and
  carried on it through the lurch: the vases show one moment, the thrust.
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).
