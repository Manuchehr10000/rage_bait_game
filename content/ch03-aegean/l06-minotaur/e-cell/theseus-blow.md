## What it is

Theseus lunging, his front foot forward, the horn in his far hand (the code's far arm), his sword
thrust home into the bull. The figure of `../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`.

## Where it stands in the game

At the horn at each blow, L+76 and L+130, for 4 frames at the first and 6 at the second; then the
grip again, and after the second he stands over the heap.

## Must be right

- **The blade over the bull's body is a line of reserved clay**, as a black-figure painter incises a
  weapon over a body (the research).
- **Bloodless**: no added red at the blow, on him or on the bull (`../LEVEL.md`).
- As `../a-door/theseus-kneel.md`, "Theseus, in every pose".

## Deliberately wrong

- **Two bloodless sword blows**, where the vases show one thrust (into the side on MFA 60.1, the
  breast on BM 1843,1103.21, the neck on Getty 86.AE.60), and sometimes blood in added red.
- **The sword**, where *Epitome* 1.9 has his fists (designer, 2026-10-06).
- **Drawn, not painted** (`../LEVEL.md`, Art, 2026-10-10).

## Sources
